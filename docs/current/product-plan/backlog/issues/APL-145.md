## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-SEC-01 — Purpose and lawful authority

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Every material data flow shall record its purpose, responsible organisation, applicable authority or processing basis, recipient and retention rule. Consent-based flows shall carry the required consent; other authorised flows shall record their distinct basis.

Acceptance: A new provider or connector cannot activate without an approved data-flow record, and a user clicking confirm cannot override an absent processing basis.
