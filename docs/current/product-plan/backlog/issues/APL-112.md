## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-21 — EHR writeback and conflict handling

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

The platform shall preserve the EHR as authority for its accepted records and shall use destination-supported identifiers, conditional updates, versions or reconciliation before writeback. Conflicting updates shall not be resolved through last-write-wins unless the destination owner explicitly approves that rule for the record type.

Acceptance: Concurrent-update tests preserve both versions, prevent silent loss and route unresolved conflicts to the named operational owner.
