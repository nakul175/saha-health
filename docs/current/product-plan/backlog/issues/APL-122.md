## User / outcome

As a worker, clinician or supervisor managing care work, I need to see an accountable person or monitored queue, reason, state and due time where appropriate for each action, so that urgent active work cannot become ownerless during creation or transfer.

Bounded scope: Cover referrals, follow-ups and exceptions, preserving the responsible queue when an assignee is removed; urgency/escalation rules must come from the approved programme.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-CARE-01 — Action ownership

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Every referral, follow-up and exception shall have an accountable person or team, a due time where appropriate, a reason and a current state. Unassigned work shall enter a monitored queue.

Acceptance: No active urgent task can exist without a visible responsible queue or assignee.
