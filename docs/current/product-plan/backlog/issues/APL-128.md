## User / outcome

As a worker using an approved communication or appointment channel, I need to track delivery, opt-out and recovery separately from patient acknowledgement, so that a provider failure cannot appear as successful contact or completed care.

Bounded scope: Each channel requires independent authorisation/readiness and keeps credentials outside the agent; failures create accountable retry or follow-up work rather than a false acknowledgement.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-CARE-07 — External messaging and scheduling

Priority: Should; original release: R2.

Approved SMS, IVR, messaging or appointment services may be integrated with authorisation, delivery evidence, opt-out handling and escalation. Channels require separate readiness checks and shall not expose provider credentials to the agent.

Acceptance: Delivery failure creates a retry or follow-up task and is not reported as patient acknowledgement.
