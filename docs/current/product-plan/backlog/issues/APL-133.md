## User / outcome

As an authorised reporting user, I want aggregation and suppression to respect the audience and approved purpose, so reports are useful without exposing small groups or restricted person records.

**Scope boundary:** Apply controls across filters and exports. Patient drilldown and exports retain separate approved access; changing filters must not evade suppression.
