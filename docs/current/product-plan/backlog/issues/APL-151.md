## User / outcome

As an operations analyst or support reviewer, I want general logs, crash reports, exports and analytics to minimise or redact sensitive content, so faults and usage can be assessed without exposing recordings, credentials or complete patient narratives.

**Scope boundary:** Necessary sensitive diagnostics require separate access and retention. An apparently safe UI does not establish safety of actual hosted telemetry.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-SEC-07 — Safe logs and analytics

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Acceptance: A representative end-to-end test leaves no raw recording, credential or complete patient narrative in general operational telemetry.
