## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-16 — Facility and professional attribution

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Where the ABDM or EHR workflow requires organisation, facility or practitioner attribution, the platform shall use verified HFR, HPR or destination identifiers and the active role. It shall not invent identifiers or reuse a professional identity across an unapproved facility context.

Acceptance: A consultation bundle fails validation or enters review when the configured facility or practitioner attribution is absent, expired or inconsistent with the encounter.
