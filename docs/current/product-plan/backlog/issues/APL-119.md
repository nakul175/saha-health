## User / outcome

As a permitted worker with a queued action, I need to have assignment, person linkage, permissions, protocol, freshness and capability status rechecked immediately before execution, so that an old draft cannot authorise a side effect after the relevant context changes.

Bounded scope: Explain review/refusal, preserve work for authorised reassignment and prevent dispatch after revoked role or material rule/data change; planning-stage checks alone do not suffice.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-OFF-06 — Revalidation of delayed actions

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Queued actions shall be revalidated against current assignment, permissions, patient linkage, protocol and input freshness before execution. A delayed clinical action shall not proceed solely because it was once drafted.

Acceptance: A material rule or data change places the action into review with an explanation instead of silently executing the old plan.

### BR-RULE-06 — Rechecks at execution time

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Permission, person linkage, capability status and relevant authorisation shall be checked immediately before an external action, even if planning occurred earlier.

Acceptance: Revoking a worker's role between drafting and execution blocks the pending action and preserves it for authorised reassignment.

## Additional CWO acceptance / refusal criteria

* **CWO-006-NEG02 (Expected refusal / recovery)**: Revoked authority or a changed record version causes a scoped refusal/reconciliation path, not a forced overwrite or fresh-key bypass.
