## User / outcome

As a permitted reviewer of inbound changes, I need to compare source-versioned external updates with pending local corrections, so that an older or conflicting update cannot silently discard local work.

Bounded scope: Preserve source, version and effective time; route conflicts to review and reconcile outstanding receipts on the connector's approved schedule.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-06 — Inbound changes and reconciliation

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Approved inbound updates shall identify source, version and effective time. Conflicts with local work shall enter a review process, and outstanding receipts shall be reconciled on a schedule appropriate to the connector.

Acceptance: An external status change does not silently discard an unsynchronised local correction, and the responsible reviewer sees both.
