## User / outcome

As a person or authorised representative considering an optional use, I want an understandable reviewed notice in an appropriate language and a recorded scoped decision, so I can decline optional AI recording while the approved non-AI care route remains available.

**Scope boundary:** Record notice version, authority, scope, time and expiry when required. Refusal must prevent the media transfer and must not remove essential service steps.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-SEC-02 — Understandable notice and consent

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Where consent is required, the workflow shall present a reviewed notice in an appropriate language and record scope, notice version, person or authorised representative, decision, time and expiry. Optional use shall be distinguishable from essential service steps.

Acceptance: Declining an optional AI recording leaves the approved non-AI care workflow available and prevents that media from being sent.
