## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-19 — Health Information User operation

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

When operating as or through an HIU, the platform shall request the minimum health information needed for the approved purpose, track consent and data-flow states, validate received bundles, preserve external provenance and prevent silent overwriting of local records.

Acceptance: A received record is shown with its source and effective date, while invalid, duplicate or conflicting content enters the configured reconciliation path.
