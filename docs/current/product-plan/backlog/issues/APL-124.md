## User / outcome

As a worker, clinician or supervisor tracking a referral, I need to distinguish proposal, authorisation, dispatch, receipt, scheduling, attendance and outcome, so that a booked appointment or sent message cannot be mistaken for completed care.

Bounded scope: Supported transitions retain defined evidence and verifying actor; unsupported closure is refused and missing attendance/result leaves the referral open or explicitly unresolved.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-CARE-03 — Referral lifecycle

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

The platform shall distinguish proposed, authorised, sent, received, scheduled, attended, completed, declined, cancelled and unknown outcomes where supported. A booked appointment or sent message shall not equal completed care.

Acceptance: Completion requires a defined evidence source and actor; unsupported closure attempts are rejected.
