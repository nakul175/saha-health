## User / outcome

As an authorised auditor, I want durable evidence of material reads, writes, approvals, AI calls, exports, configuration and privileged access with actor, authority, object, time, outcome and version, so an encounter or exchange can be reconstructed independently.

**Scope boundary:** Ordinary users cannot alter audit history. Designated external writes and privileged changes stop if durable audit cannot be established; local capture retains its permitted local queue. Exchanges must be traced without raw clinical payloads in general logs.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-SEC-08 — Audit evidence

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

The platform shall record material reads, writes, approvals, AI calls, exports, configuration changes and privileged access with actor, authority, affected object, time, outcome and version. Ordinary users shall not be able to alter audit history.

Acceptance: An auditor reconstructs a selected encounter and submission from evidence without relying on the chat transcript or provider recollection.

### NFR-10 — Audit completeness and timing

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

All mandatory audit events shall be durable before or atomically with the consequential action where feasible. If durable audit cannot be established, external writes and privileged changes shall stop. Local capture may continue with its local audit queue.

Verification: Audit-store failure blocks the designated actions, while recovery preserves event order and detects clock skew.

### NFR-26 — Consent and exchange traceability

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Every ABDM or direct EHR exchange shall be traceable from user purpose and authority through request, transformation, disclosure or receipt, acknowledgement and final state without placing raw clinical payloads in general application logs.

Acceptance: Verification: Audit reconstruction succeeds for all release exchanges, while log inspection confirms that secrets, tokens and unnecessary clinical content are absent.
