## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-OPS-07 — Data exit and continuity

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

The buyer shall be able to export its authorised operational records, configuration and audit evidence in documented formats under the agreement. Provider or platform termination shall include queue disposition and care-task handover.

Acceptance: An exit rehearsal produces a usable export, a list of unresolved external actions and a named owner for open patient tasks.

### NFR-22 — Data portability and export integrity

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Authorised structured records shall be exportable with identifiers, relationships, units, provenance and version metadata. Export format documentation shall enable an independent party to interpret the data.

Verification: A 1000-person test export is re-imported into a validation environment with complete record counts and relationships.
