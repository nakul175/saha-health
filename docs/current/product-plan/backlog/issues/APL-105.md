## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-11 — Canonical exchange representation

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

The platform shall maintain a canonical representation for person, encounter, observation, condition, service request, care plan, medication, diagnostic result, document and provenance. Source payloads and destination payloads shall remain traceable to this representation without erasing material differences.

Acceptance: A single confirmed blood-pressure observation can be mapped to two approved destinations, and the audit shows the original source, canonical value, units, mapping version and each destination payload.
