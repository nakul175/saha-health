## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-15 — ABDM consent enforcement

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

For ABDM exchanges that require a consent artefact, the platform shall enforce the named HIU and HIP, purpose, health information types, date range, expiry and status. Revocation, expiry or mismatch shall stop new retrieval or disclosure and create an auditable outcome.

Acceptance: Requests outside the approved purpose, date range, data type or validity period are blocked even when the calling user can otherwise view the local patient record.
