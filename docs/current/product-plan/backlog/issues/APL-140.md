## User / outcome

As an accountable support operator, I want scoped alerts and redacted traces for platform, connector, queue, model and permission failures, so the right owner can respond and reconstruct the affected task.

**Scope boundary:** Retain the source alert targets and minimise clinical content in traces. A five-minute scheduler setting or simulated message is not measured alert delivery; no paid plan or new recipient is authorised.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-OPS-04 — Operational monitoring

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Operators shall monitor platform health, connector failures, pending queues, model availability, permission anomalies and costs. Alerts shall identify severity, affected scope, accountable owner and required response.

Acceptance: An induced provider outage and a stalled writeback queue reach the correct support owner within the agreed alert target.

### NFR-18 — Operational observability

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Major platform, queue or connector failures shall generate an actionable alert within 5 minutes of detection criteria being met. Traces shall link a user task to provider calls and destination receipts without exposing unnecessary clinical content.

Verification: Induced failures reach the correct queue and permit reconstruction using redacted diagnostic data.
