## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-09 — Per-destination data minimisation

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Each connector shall send only authorised fields needed by its contract. Person-level operational data shall not be sent to an aggregate-reporting system merely because both are labelled health MIS.

Acceptance: Payload fixtures and production audits verify field allowlists and distinguish person records from aggregate measures.
