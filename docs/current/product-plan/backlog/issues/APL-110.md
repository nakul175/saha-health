## User / outcome

As an integration owner for an approved HIP deployment, I need to publish eligible records and fulfil only authorised care-context requests through the approved exchange, so that request, encryption, transfer, acknowledgement and failure evidence remain traceable without exposing unrelated records.

Bounded scope: Care-context linkage and consent scope constrain every disclosure; this item does not enable a HIP role or a real exchange provider by itself.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-18 — Health Information Provider operation

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

When operating as or through a HIP, the platform shall create eligible records, preserve care-context linkage, respond only through authorised exchange, encrypt and transmit the required payload, and retain the ABDM acknowledgement and failure state.

Acceptance: A consented request retrieves only the authorised records and produces traceable request, encryption, transfer, acknowledgement and error evidence without exposing unrelated records.
