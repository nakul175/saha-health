## User / outcome

As a worker needing help and an authorised support agent, I want a failed task to produce a support case with redacted diagnostics, so routine faults can be diagnosed without unrestricted patient access.

**Scope boundary:** Exceptional content access must have an approved purpose, scope and duration, be logged and expire. A support role alone must not grant broad record access.

### BR-OPS-05 — Support without unnecessary disclosure

Priority: Must; original release: R1.
