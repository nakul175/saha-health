## User / outcome

As an integration owner, I need to declare the exact approved ABDM roles and operations for each deployment, so that enabling one module cannot grant another module's capabilities.

Bounded scope: Keep role, environment, facility, credentials and responsible-owner evidence explicit; ABHA-only configuration cannot retrieve as an HIU, and unapproved operations remain unavailable.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-13 — ABDM participation scope

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Each deployment shall explicitly approve the ABDM modules and roles it uses, including any ABHA, Scan and Share, HIP, HIU, consented exchange or NHCX capability. Permission for one module or role shall not imply another.

Acceptance: Administration and test evidence identify the enabled ABDM role, environment, facility, client credentials, approved operations and responsible owner; all other operations are unavailable.
