## Original requirement and acceptance text

### NFR-03 — Visible AI progress

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

An online AI task shall acknowledge its state within 2 seconds and display progress or an honest pending state. Each capability shall have an approved completion deadline; a default interactive deadline of 30 seconds is proposed, with longer asynchronous tasks clearly identified.
