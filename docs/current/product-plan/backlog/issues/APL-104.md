As an integration owner, I need to validate outbound clinical artefacts against the pinned agreed schema, profiles, terminology and business rules, so that invalid content is rejected before external submission with a specific error.

Bounded scope: Keep validator, package, mappings, policy-accepted warnings and upgrade evidence versioned; preserve the source's 100 percent pre-submission validation target, and distinguish conformance from owner acceptance.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-INT-10 — Standards-based exchange

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

Where an ecosystem requires a published standard or implementation guide, the connector shall use a pinned agreed version and pass its conformance checks. ABDM exchange shall follow the applicable onboarding flows and the current agreed ABDM FHIR profiles \[S1, S2, S8, S9\].

Acceptance: Standards validation and owner acceptance are recorded separately; valid FHIR content alone does not mark the connector production-ready.

### BR-INT-17 — ABDM FHIR conformance and version control

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

ABDM clinical artefacts shall conform to the pinned NRCeS FHIR implementation package agreed for the deployment. The validator, profile set, terminology content and mapping shall be versioned together; upgrades require regression testing and owner approval \[S2\].

Acceptance: Every outbound test bundle passes the pinned validator; a deliberate profile violation is rejected with a specific error and no external submission.

### NFR-25 — Clinical exchange validation

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

One hundred percent of outbound structured clinical exchange payloads shall pass the pinned schema, profile, terminology and business-rule checks before submission. Validation warnings that are accepted by policy shall remain visible and versioned.

Acceptance: Verification: The release corpus covers every supported artefact and includes invalid cardinality, code, reference, unit, signature and bundle cases.
