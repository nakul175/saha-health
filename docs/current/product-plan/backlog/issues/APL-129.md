## User / outcome

As a worker or supervisor reviewing programme activity evidence, I need to distinguish performed, claimed, verified and submitted work with its correction history, so that reporting and incentive-support evidence stays reviewable without promising payment.

Bounded scope: A cancelled or corrected activity updates derived claims, retains the original evidence and marks prepared incentive evidence stale; scheme rates, payment and field verification remain separate pending requirements.

## Original requirement and acceptance text

### BR-CARE-08 — Worker activity evidence

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

The system shall generate a reviewable record of verified worker activities for programme reporting or incentive support. It shall distinguish performed, claimed, verified and submitted activity and avoid promising payment.

Acceptance: Correcting or cancelling an activity updates derived claims and leaves the original evidence and review history traceable.
