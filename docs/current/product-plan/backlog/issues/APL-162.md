## Original requirement and acceptance text

### NFR-02 — Responsive field interface

Priority: Must; original release: R1.

On the reference device, local field saves shall complete within 500 ms at the 95th percentile and cached patient or task screens within 2 seconds. Connected core screens shall target 3 seconds at the 95th percentile under the reference network.

Verification: Instrument representative tasks under R1 load and report failed requests and slow outliers, not just averages.
