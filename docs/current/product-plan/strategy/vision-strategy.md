## 1. Direction and decision now

**Vision:** Help ASHAs complete their existing routine community-health work through one clear mobile journey, with less repeated entry and reliable records across connectivity interruptions, while preserving existing responsibilities, supervision and approvals.

**Strategy:** Prove a small manual assigned-person journey first; make saving and interruption states trustworthy; then add reusable programme capture, bounded assistance and authorised exchange without making routine work depend on an AI provider. The immediate investment is the existing D03 review/save candidate after a fresh bounded baseline decision. D02 already supplies the historical assigned-Today/read-only witness. A complete full-product plan is the navigation for later work; it is not permission to activate the whole product at once.

The user's latest instruction authorises documentation improvement with clearly labelled reasonable assumptions instead of repeated questions. It does not establish missing external approvals, participants, contractual rights, provider access or measured outcomes. Recommendations below are usable planning defaults; an affected implementation or production decision must satisfy its actual gate.

## 2. Product, people and operating context

| Group | Job and value sought | Authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individually signed-in ASHA | Find the correct assigned existing person; perform the permitted routine task; understand whether work is prepared, saved, pending or confirmed | Server identity, assignment, purpose and current role govern access. Owner access cannot enlarge patient scope. |
| Clinician | Review or act within the existing qualified clinical workflow | Clinical judgement and escalation remain with authorised clinical roles; model output cannot create that authority. |
| Supervisor / programme manager | Oversee permitted work and programme outcomes without imposing invented worker approvals | Use existing approved responsibilities and purpose-limited reports. |
| Organisation administrator | Configure authorised workforce, programme and access context | Administration is distinct from permission to read every person's record. |
| Permitted provider / system-owner teams | Receive or supply only owner-authorised information and evidence | A transport success is distinct from independent programme acceptance or a clinical conclusion. |
| Operators / support | Identify the release, diagnose incidents and recover authorised service | Use current approved topology and recovery authority; a documentation update grants no new hosting or live recovery permission. |
| People whose information is recorded | Accurate, appropriately scoped records and purpose-limited handling | No real patient data is used in the currently authorised demonstrations. Production rights, notices and processing decisions need the relevant qualified owner. |

The first working scope is synthetic/training ASHA work on assigned existing people. Simulated phone viewports demonstrate responsive behavior; actual handset, local-language and frontline suitability need separate evidence. Full target scope includes governed programme configuration, clinical oversight, optional AI, MIS/EHR/ABDM exchange, offline continuity, care coordination, reporting, security and release/support operations.

## 4. Strategic choices and trade-offs

| Choice | Why now | Cost / trade-off | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start with correct existing person and explicit review/save | A wrong person or misleading save state undermines every later capability; uses established server contracts | Narrow first demonstration omits full scheduling, programme, language and workflow breadth | Current scope passes and frontline evidence identifies the next material friction. |
| Complete one bounded implementation at a time | Makes the final diff, test contract, merge and hosted evidence assessable | Fewer concurrent delivery claims; discovery and documentation can still proceed independently | The single-controller handoff/failure-resume protocol is proven, without creating competing D03 work. |
| Establish manual success before optional AI | Provides a fallback and meaningful comparator; limits authority and provider dependence | AI breadth is deferred from the first coding package, not removed from R1 | Permitted task, evaluation contract, actual provider/data authority and G3 prerequisites exist for the exact capability. |
| Protect interrupted work before claiming field continuity | A local acknowledgement, queued action and independent destination receipt are different states | Synthetic proof is bounded; production policy and actual phone testing remain necessary | DEC-03 and relevant device/privacy/recovery decisions are resolved and tested. |
| Reuse individually verified existing components | Avoids parallel stores and unreviewable bulk changes; retains authoritative identity, version, audit and receipt rules | Inspection can reveal inherited defects requiring repair or a narrower scope | Each current-code change, stale dependency or evidence failure triggers fresh impact review. |
| Keep original BRD targets and release labels | Preserves accountable scope and makes remaining work visible | A small delivered task does not make its parent or R1 green | Only a legitimate versioned product decision can change a requirement; no weakening to get a pass. |
| Separate learning from delivery | Allows a technically successful feature to be reconsidered if user benefit is absent | Requires a later measured review and decision, not only issue closure | Every release-learning checkpoint and material incident. |

The current alternatives are recorded, not silently discarded: broad provider-first integration; activating the old monolithic UI; bulk-merging old online/offline PRs; treating local save as completed programme exchange. They carry broader dependencies or unacceptable current evidence/authority gaps. Preserve their source artifacts; select verified components only after inspection.

## 6. Priority and sizing policy

**Recommended operating policy PM-PRIORITY-01, revision 1:** retain original source priorities. For a ready execution choice, apply a dependency and safety screen first; then compare user value, frequency/reach evidence, urgency, reduction of a material uncertainty or failure, incremental cost, and opportunity cost. Write the comparison with the next plausible alternative. Do not turn unsupported 1–5 ratings into a fabricated RICE score or imply numerical precision without actual reach/impact evidence.

The immediate ordered choice is D01 bounded baseline decision → qualify existing D03 → D04 → D05. D07 evidence supports release boundaries; D08 actual device/frontline evidence supports the claims that require it; D06 follows a stable permitted manual path. This protects work already prepared and tests failure handling before expanding automation. The full work-package dependencies and R1/R2 goals remain in the roadmap.

**Recommended sizing policy PM-SIZE-01, revision 1:** estimate inspected work in ideal engineering days with a low/most-likely/high range, split implementation from qualification, state assumptions/dependencies and record the actual estimator/date/confidence. An epic estimate is a range over its scoped ready slices and discovery uncertainty, not a sum of invented unrefined story points. Treat estimates as forecasts, never acceptance criteria or commitments. Recommended low-confidence triage/engineering ranges may be added to backlog descriptions as planning assumptions; they are distinct from an inspected estimate and do not update actual estimate fields, capacity or target dates. No source-backed capacity or qualified sizing currently exists for the whole backlog. Tomorrow's first inspected sizing output is the remaining D03 qualification effort after diff/baseline inspection; the complete product has no invented completion date.

For epics or later stories without sufficient inspection, mark an actual inspected estimate as unresolved and name the discovery/current-code inspection needed before execution. Any supplied planning range must retain its method, uncertainty and assumptions and cannot pass as actual team capacity or a commitment. This is an explicit readiness input, not a missing field hidden by template prose.

## 7. Outcome measurement contract

Recommended measurement record `SAHA-MEASUREMENT-01`, revision 1. Preserve the source BRD proposed targets and their approval status; this table defines collection and interpretation, not replacement targets. Target decisions live in source D15; frontline benefit cannot be accepted until a comparable baseline and predeclared target exist.

| Outcome / metric | Exact unit and denominator | Baseline / collection plan | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Correct assigned-person work | Exact-person list/open/readback cases passed divided by attempted applicable cases; report every wrong-record, assignment and role refusal separately | Synthetic server/UI fixtures on declared candidate and authority versions; existing D02 evidence is the historic comparator | Every frozen identity/access invariant must pass. Zero observed wrong-record or unauthorised access failures in the declared suite; finite tests are not proof of impossibility. |
| Routine task completion | Completed eligible attempts / all eligible attempts, including failed and abandoned attempts; separate manual and assisted paths | Same task definition, inputs and workflow context for current process and candidate; log failure reason and interruption | Compare against the predeclared approved outcome target; an empty denominator is inconclusive. Synthetic completion qualifies mechanics only. |
| Less repeated entry | Repeated entries / eligible reuse opportunities, with the eligible opportunity rubric fixed before observing the candidate | Observe original and candidate capture; differentiate reused values from time-specific attestation and newly required questions | Source D13 work retirement and D15 measures require buyer/product evidence; faster capture alone cannot retire official work. |
| Easier work | Active task time plus total elapsed time for all comparable attempts; completion rate reported alongside | Record start/stop boundaries, assistance, failed attempts, interrupts and retries; retain raw or permitted summarised observations | Do not report “time saved” from successful cases alone or compare a small synthetic form to the full original journey. |
| Honest saving | Original applicable encrypted-local-save p95 **≤500 ms**; separately report connected-server save/readback timing | Freeze original timing boundary, N, device/storage/data profile and fault counts before the run; preserve failed receipts | DEC-01 forbids replacing 500 ms with the unapproved one-second proposal or changing the profile after a failure. |
| Durable continuity | Lost acknowledged writes / faulted acknowledged writes; duplicate supported effects / replay/reconnect attempts; unresolved queue age and outcomes | Declared interruption, restart, acknowledgement-drop, version conflict and replay fixtures; preserve exact operation identity | Mandatory fault cases must show the declared durable outcome. Local queued state cannot claim remote acceptance. |
| Phone usability | Cases passed / applicable cases per viewport and separately per physical handset/browser/language fixture | Existing declared 360×640/taller, 200% text, focus/labels/44px targets; actual keyboards, restart, memory and battery require real device evidence | Simulated viewport success does not close D08 physical-phone/frontline acceptance. |
| Assistance value and safety | Exact capability metric, costly-error/refusal rates, per-slice numerators/denominators and manual-path success | Link versioned assurance record and frozen dataset/configuration; evaluate only within actual data/provider permissions | No generic X% certifies every AI capability. Frozen per-capability thresholds and zero permitted authority leaks govern its gate. |

Every record includes metric ID/version, source criterion, baseline, target/operator, eligible population, numerator/denominator, exclusions, fixture, observation window, candidate/configuration, uncertainty and accountable reviewer. Missing targets, observations or owners stay explicit; do not fill them with favourable facts. Nakul Jain coordinates product measurement and frontline validation; authorised programme/clinical/privacy/security/device owners must be established where their decision is required.

## 8. Discovery and learning

Use the linked **AI assurance, discovery and learning contract** for each epic's riskiest assumption, minimum useful disconfirmation test and effect on priority. Start with source/code inspection and synthetic evidence now. Participant recruitment, observation of real clinical work or external provider experiments require their actual permissions; this document does not authorise them.

After each qualified bounded release, review exact release identity, observed engineering behavior, incidents and unresolved defects. Schedule a later product-outcome review when authorised comparable user evidence exists. Recommend the first engineering review after the next bounded synthetic release and a product review after the first complete permitted baseline/candidate observation window; these are trigger-based review obligations, not a new timer or automation. Choose continue, improve, narrow, roll back or retire within actual authority. Link the decision to strategy, roadmap, owning items and future tests. An absent or undersized sample results in “insufficient evidence,” never “benefit proved.”

Reopen strategy when wrong-record access, misleading success, acknowledged work loss or an authority leak is observed; when assumptions are disproved; or when target outcomes fail despite technical delivery. A harmful effect cannot be balanced away by aggregate task success. Preserve the failed evidence and original acceptance before proposing changes.
