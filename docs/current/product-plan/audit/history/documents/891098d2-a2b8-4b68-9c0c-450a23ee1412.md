# SAHA product vision and strategy

Planning baseline v1 · 10 October 2026 · organised at sponsor request before further coding.

## Vision

Help ASHAs complete existing routine community-health work through one clear mobile journey, with less repeated entry and reliable records across connectivity interruptions, while preserving existing responsibilities, supervision and approvals.

## Full product and users

The full product connects routine community-worker work with governed programme configuration, clinical oversight, optional AI capabilities, MIS/EHR/ABDM exchange, offline continuity, care coordination, reporting, security and release/support operations. ASHAs are the frontline entry point; clinicians, supervisors/programme managers, organisation administrators, permitted provider teams and operators have distinct scoped responsibilities. Authority remains role/purpose/assignment bound; optional assistance never substitutes for authorised clinical decisions.

## Primary user and problem

Primary user: an individually signed-in ASHA working on her assigned existing people. The problem to validate with frontline workers is repeated data entry and fragmented workflow, compounded by uncertain saving and connectivity. Synthetic demonstrations establish engineering behaviour; they do not establish ASHA adoption, clinical benefit or reduced task time.

## First delivery sequence

1. Make the assigned-person and bounded manual routine journey work first: D01–D03.
2. Protect prepared work through offline interruption and reconnect: D04–D05.
3. Add bounded assistance only over working permitted actions: D06. Manual work must remain usable without a model.
4. Qualify compact-phone/frontline conditions: D08. Gather actual device and ASHA evidence separately from simulated viewports.
5. Carry exact release identity and authorised recovery evidence through delivery: D07.

Reuse individually verified current components. Preserve authoritative server identity, assignment, purpose, version, audit and receipt rules. Work one story at a time; finish its declared gate before advancing. Existing parent BRD/scope requirements remain unchanged.

## Strategic choices and validation

The first investment is a small manual assigned-person journey with truthful review/save states. It uses the existing authoritative server identity, assignment and audited record workflow. This gives a testable baseline before optional assistance, integrations or wider programme scope. D02 provides the bounded list/open witness; D03 remains the held review/save candidate.

The next investment protects prepared work across interruption and reconnect before making stronger field-continuity claims. Reuse and independent destination receipts reduce repeated capture without treating a local save as external programme acceptance. Optional assistance is added only over permitted working actions; manual routine work stays usable during model/provider failure. Full R1 clinical/AI/integration obligations remain in the target scope and need their own authorised gates.

These choices test three product assumptions: workers can select the correct assigned person and understand the save state; reusing eligible information reduces repeat entry; declared interruption/recovery behaviour fits actual phone and field conditions. Frontline observations, approved workflow context and device access are still needed. Synthetic passes alone cannot confirm those assumptions. Wrong-record access, misleading success, lost acknowledged work or an authority leak prevents qualification of the affected behaviour. Research findings can change prioritisation through an explicit product decision while retaining original source criteria and traceability.

## Product success and measurement

| Outcome | Measurement and acceptance boundary |
| -- | -- |
| Less repeated entry | Record repeated entries divided by eligible reuse opportunities in the observed tasks. Declare what counts as an eligible opportunity and exclude genuinely different time/attestation questions with reasons. Target, observation window and comparable baseline require frontline/product agreement. |
| Easier routine work | Report completed attempts divided by all eligible attempts, plus active task time for comparable baseline and candidate tasks. Retain failed/incomplete attempts, interruptions and their elapsed time separately; do not calculate improvement from successful attempts alone. |
| Trustworthy records | Report exact-person/provenance/save-state checks passed over checks attempted on the declared role, assignment and version fixtures; retain every authorization/refusal/error failure. User understanding of save states requires separate observed ASHA evidence. |
| Reliable continuity | Record acknowledged writes lost over acknowledged writes subjected to the declared fault, and duplicate supported actions over replay/reconnect attempts. Report queue age, unresolved outcomes, device/storage fixture, observation period and excluded failure modes; a local acknowledgement is not a destination receipt. |
| Usable phones | Story checks include declared 360×640/taller viewports, 200% text, focus and labelled 44px targets. Report each actual handset/browser/language fixture separately for keyboard, restart, memory and battery checks; simulated viewports cannot supply physical-phone or actual-worker usability evidence. |
| Honest saving | Original applicable local-save p95 ≤500 ms target stays unwaived. Declare the original timing boundary, sample count, device/data/storage fixture and failure count before measurement. Encrypted local and connected-server timing remain different measurements; do not change the profile after failure. |

Engineering evidence is reported by exact code/configuration and test selection. Every measurement records its source/version, fixture, observation window, denominator, exclusions and accountable reviewer. Nakul Jain coordinates product measurement and frontline validation; the authorised organisation must name applicable programme/clinical/security/device owners. No benefit target, participant count or approval is invented here. Formal UAT currently 0/364; a narrower delivery gate never accepts the full parent requirement.
