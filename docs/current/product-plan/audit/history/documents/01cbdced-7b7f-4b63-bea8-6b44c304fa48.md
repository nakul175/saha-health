Use with the central quality-checker and improvement-loop contract. These are documentation checks, not approval of requirements, execution readiness or product delivery.

Every applicable check must pass. Required unknowns remain unresolved; never invent a target, owner, date, approval or evidence to clear a check. Shared field rules also apply to every hierarchy element. The complete reusable catalogue also retains failure examples and repair/question prompts for every check.
