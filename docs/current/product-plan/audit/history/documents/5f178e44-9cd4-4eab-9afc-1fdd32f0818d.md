## Increment/dependency roadmap

Preserve the original sequence: (1) identity and safe records WP01–WP03; (2) programme reuse WP04; (3) bounded AI/clinical oversight WP05–WP06; (4) destination exchange WP07; (5) field continuity WP08–WP09; (6) operations/pilot WP10–WP11. Dependency work and external discovery can be planned without activating providers or promising integration acceptance. These increments describe target outcomes, not six delivered releases. Work-package links express design dependencies; they do not require completing every upstream epic before a bounded task can reuse individually verified components. The first manual D01–D03 journey and D04/D05 continuity queue remain the implementation order. They do not depend on activating AI or external destinations first. A future task freezes only the current-code prerequisites actually required by its declared boundary; full R1 gates retain the wider clinical, integration and production obligations.
