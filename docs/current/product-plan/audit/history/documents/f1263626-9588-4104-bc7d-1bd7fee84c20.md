Version 1.0 · 10 October 2026 · field-level quality criteria.

Use with the central quality-checker and improvement-loop contract. These are documentation checks, not approval of requirements, execution readiness or product delivery.

Every applicable check must pass. Required unknowns remain unresolved; never invent a target, owner, date, approval or evidence to clear a check. Shared field rules also apply to every hierarchy element. The complete reusable catalogue also retains failure examples and repair/question prompts for every check.

11 fields in this section. Source bullets retain their original locations; derived details identify their explicit source anchor. These are check definitions, not additional Linear backlog records.

## Whole-element and cross-record checks

**hierarchy.01** · traceability · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Each instantiated record has a stable canonical identity and resolves to the correct parent level; acceptance criteria remain sections in owning items and tasks retain canonical owner links.
* **Evidence:** Canonical ID and source clause/version.

**hierarchy.02** · nonredundancy · review · priority major

* **Pass:** No canonical item/source criterion is duplicated by views, release allocation, cross-epic links, related contributors or shared tests.
* **Evidence:** Canonical owner and unique counts.

**hierarchy.03** · consistency · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Higher-level outcomes, lower-level scope and states agree or differ through an explicitly resolved authorized decision. Any unresolved contradiction blocks the affected quality gate.
* **Evidence:** Related scope/state/source records.

**hierarchy.04** · completeness · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Declared full-scope source clauses/cases have canonical owner/criterion mapping or visible gaps; uncovered original scope prevents false completion.
* **Evidence:** Field and required details; scoped exclusions.

**hierarchy.05** · versioning · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Original requirements/cases and frozen specifications remain immutable/recoverable; substantive proposals have separate revisions/authority and affected evidence invalidation.
* **Evidence:** Current revision/change authority.

**hierarchy.06** · authorization · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Source provenance, specification approval and execution result are separate through every rollup and transition.
* **Evidence:** Authority and scoped decision/access.

**hierarchy.07** · feasibility · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Concrete completion prerequisites form a valid actionable graph with identifiable required outputs and no unresolvable cycles; nonblocking design guidance does not create blanket blocking.
* **Evidence:** Constraints/access/predecessor outputs.

**hierarchy.08** · operability · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Every material unresolved decision/blocker names its missing input/evidence, accountable authority or explicit owner gap, and exact resumption condition.
* **Evidence:** Failure/recovery owner/state/resume.

**hierarchy.09** · measurability · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Progress numerators/denominators enumerate unique canonical criteria/items for fixed scope/spec versions; no task/assertion count substitutes for acceptance.
* **Evidence:** Definition/population/method/source.

**hierarchy.10** · evidence_integrity · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Evidence authenticity, originating artifact, environment, version and observation time are sufficient for every material acceptance/delivery claim.
* **Evidence:** Original output/version/context/time.

**hierarchy.11** · evidence_integrity · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** A change to relevant source/spec/code/environment invalidates dependent checks/reviews/results until rechecked; superseded evidence remains historical.
* **Evidence:** Original output/version/context/time.

**hierarchy.12** · authorization · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Actual branch protections, required checks and independent human approval requirements are honoured; no bypass or fabricated approvals.
* **Evidence:** Authority and scoped decision/access.

**hierarchy.13** · evidence_integrity · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Code qualification, PR head, merge SHA, actually observed deployed SHA and live synthetic journey results are separate and compared where applicable.
* **Evidence:** Original output/version/context/time.

**hierarchy.14** · privacy · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Synthetic fixtures and evidence minimize data and cannot contact real recipients or expose real patient records/secrets; authority boundaries are preserved.
* **Evidence:** Approved purpose and minimized fixture.

**hierarchy.15** · security · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Role/record/session/assignment/API boundaries derive from authoritative server identity/scoping and relevant positive/refusal/adverse checks.
* **Evidence:** Authoritative boundary/adverse evidence.

**hierarchy.16** · authorization · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Planning/templates/review do not authorize new patient databases, visits, providers, paid services, hosting, clinical policies or destructive migrations.
* **Evidence:** Authority and scoped decision/access.

**hierarchy.17** · learning · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Questions resolve substantive missing information; answers include provenance and authority and are not inferred from silence, time, repetition or model confidence.
* **Evidence:** Hypothesis/method/decision rule.

**hierarchy.18** · clarity · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Terminology, actor names, units, abbreviations, state labels and record identity have one defined meaning across levels; synonyms do not hide conflicts.
* **Evidence:** Wording, definitions and referents.

**hierarchy.19** · prioritization · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Sequence and deferrals preserve priority rationale and gate evidence; held work does not advance merely because templates passed editorial review.
* **Evidence:** Order/trade-off/constraint rationale.

**hierarchy.20** · user_value · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Every lower-level outcome/output traces to a user/operator need and higher-level goal; orphan activity with no contribution is removed, linked or explicitly justified.
* **Evidence:** Actor need and outcome link.

**hierarchy.21** · scope_atomicity · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Scope boundaries and hand-offs give one owning outcome per item, with shared contributions explicit and no hidden activation or unrelated bundled change.
* **Evidence:** Outcome boundary/exclusions/hand-offs.

**hierarchy.22** · testability · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Relevant positive/refusal/error/authorization/recovery/NFR scenarios are covered by reproducible synthetic verification or explicit source-based nonapplicability.
* **Evidence:** Fixture/action/result/test mapping.

**hierarchy.23** · accessibility · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Applicable device/input/accessibility obligations from the source/design authority are preserved and mapped to observable checks.
* **Evidence:** Source standard and interaction check.

**hierarchy.24** · operability · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Relevant rollout/recovery/audit/retention/offline/concurrency obligations have canonical contract/authority and realistic failure/recovery verification.
* **Evidence:** Failure/recovery owner/state/resume.

**hierarchy.25** · authorization · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Conditional N/A is scoped, independently reviewed and supported by factual context; an unknown answer, missing source, failed test or inconvenient requirement cannot become N/A.
* **Evidence:** Authority and scoped decision/access.

**hierarchy.26** · evidence_integrity · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** No score, average, generated self-review, iteration limit or lack of new questions yields PASS while any applicable required check is unresolved, failed, blocked or unreviewed.
* **Evidence:** Original output/version/context/time.

**hierarchy.27** · versioning · review · priority critical

* **Pass:** Independent review is bound to the exact current artifact fingerprint and attributable to a reviewer distinct from the author; edits invalidate dependent attestations.
* **Evidence:** Current revision/change authority.

**hierarchy.28** · consistency · review · priority major

* **Pass:** Documentation quality PASS is distinguished from product Ready, story Accepted and release Delivered; each workflow retains its own source-defined gate.
* **Evidence:** Related scope/state/source records.
