# Development slice 2e — who recorded each measurement, and its verification

30 September 2026 (IST). Builds on slice 2c. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged.

## What changed

- **Author for each measurement.** Each of the five slice 2c measurements (blood pressure, weight, temperature, gestational age, cough duration) now carries who recorded it: account id, display name, account code, role at the time and the save time. The author comes from the authenticated server identity and the member's current role, never from the request: the facts object is strict and refuses an author field, and a command-level author field is dropped. After every command the server compares each visit's facts with their previous state (for a new correction draft, with the visit it corrects) and credits a measurement to the actor when its value or its own source/time entry changed. An unchanged measurement keeps its author; a cleared one loses it. This covers the online save, field sync, field registration, assistant field application and correction drafts through one rule in `lib/saha/fact-verification.ts`.
- **Older facts are not attributed.** A measurement saved before this slice, or left unchanged since, shows "Recorded by: not recorded". Nothing guesses an author from the visit's creator or confirmer.
- **Verify or dispute.** A new patient command, `verifyFact`, lets a clinician verify or dispute one measurement on a current confirmed visit. The server requires the clinician role and the patient in the clinician's care scope (emergency access does not permit it), a current confirmed visit that was not corrected or voided, the visit's current record version, a measurement that was recorded (a value or a missing reason), a reason of at least 10 characters for a dispute, a different clinician from the one who recorded the measurement, and a decision that differs from the current state. Replays recheck role and scope under the current membership.
- **Corrections append.** Reviews are appended to the visit (`factReviews`: measurement, state, clinician, time, reason); the latest one for a measurement is its state, and earlier ones stay. Each review changes the visit record, so the store writes a record revision, and it is audited with the patient id. The facts and the visit version do not change, so existing plans and protocol reviews stay valid. A correction draft keeps the authors of copied facts and starts unverified.
- **Visit screen.** The review tab lists, for each recorded measurement, who recorded it and its verification state ("unverified", "verified by … (clinician), time", or "disputed by …, time: reason"). Clinicians in scope get Verify and Dispute buttons on confirmed visits (a dispute asks for the reason); their own measurements show a note that another clinician must review them.
- **FHIR.** Both exports carry the recorder and verification state only where the pinned rule set `saha-fhir-r4-document-structure` 1.0.0 already accepts it, which needed no change to the rule set: in the FHIR document (EHR publication and mock ABDM transfer) an observation's `performer` references a `Practitioner` for the recorder when one is known (otherwise the document author, as before), and each observation note adds "Recorded by: … Verification: …". Gestational age and cough duration lines in the narrative add the same text. The FHIR example download adds the same note text. Visits with no recorded authors and no reviews produce exactly the earlier output.

## Limits

- The author is whoever last saved a change to a measurement's value or its own source/time entry. Changing only the visit-level source or time does not re-credit measurements that inherit them.
- Verification is by clinicians only and is per measurement; visit notes and programme answers are not covered. There is no supervisor sign-off, no required verification before sending, and a dispute does not block plans, prepared publications or programme submissions.
- Reviews cannot be withdrawn to "unverified"; a later review replaces the state and both stay in history. The record history shows the review list as changed data, not as a formatted list.
- FHIR carries verification as note text; no `Provenance` resource, verification extension or coded status is emitted, because the pinned rule set does not permit them. Programme (MIS) payloads do not carry authors or verification.
- A measurement recorded in the field notebook is credited to the account that synced it, which is the notebook's signed-in worker.
