## Proposed bounded software contract (not implemented)

1. Explicit opt-in execution policy per exact installationId, manifestHash, packId/version and team, with effectiveAt UTC and explicit anchor enum confirmedAt. Owner proposes; a different currently scoped clinician reviews exact immutable policy hash; owner activates only after fresh proposer/reviewer membership version, normal care/purpose/team and installation authority checks. Default and legacy installations remain descriptive. New policy versions, suspension and replacement append history; no silent reinterpretation of old approvals or automatic clinical approval.
2. Only current confirmed, non-void, non-superseded visits captured under that exact installed manifest and active pinned episode qualify. Trusted confirmedAt UTC plus declared integer days determines due work. Future confirmations at/after policy effectiveAt materialise once; bounded scheduled catch-up repairs eligible missing occurrences after transient failure. No arbitrary date inference, retrospective source reinterpretation or external send. Due day is operational configured cadence, not a clinical denominator or invented protocol.
3. Deterministic source identity binds org/person/episode/visit, confirmed revision/content hash, policy/version, installation/hash, ruleId and occurrence kind. An append-only occurrence/event ledger retains exact UTC anchor, derived due day, declared urgency and source/replacement/invalidation provenance. A single transaction guards current source/policy/member/owner basis and writes occurrence/event/audit/receipt together. CAS failure rolls back, stable same-key replay returns an exact currently authorized receipt without recreating mutation. Clinical confirm persistence must not falsely succeed while promised work disappears; prefer the same transaction or an atomic durable execution intent with idempotent recovery, rather than an untracked post-response effect.
4. Follow-up and escalation are distinct occurrences. A true declared escalation remains unresolved regardless of unrelated task completion or lower-risk AI. Only an explicit independently scoped clinician decision with exact current source/occurrence version and reason can acknowledge/resolve it. A source correction/void appends invalidation or replacement review-required evidence; it does not erase, automatically resolve or suppress the unresolved escalation. Re-evaluation of replacement evidence is explicit and does not reinterpret the original confirmed body. The original declared rule conditions and urgency are retained; no diagnosis, prescribing or official programme registration is inferred.
5. Current normal patient scope and care-coordination purpose apply to read, action, assignment and saved receipt recovery. Assignee remains an active normally scoped care worker; revoked/withdrawn owners are visible as ownership gaps, not an authority bypass. Read audit precedes disclosure and fresh source/policy/scope recheck. UI exposes source-backed work, exact pinned rule/version, timing anchor, pending review and policy limits. Action-plan/caseload can display through a narrow source adapter without deriving work solely in a read or merging incompatible tasks.
6. Confirmation hook executes server-derived policy only, after existing confirmation/clinical verification guards and before durable persist. Scheduled hook respects maintenance, bounded page/query/time limits, continuation cursor, system audit identity and reviewed policy scope; it does not impersonate a clinician or call providers. A manual Evaluate may offer diagnosis/catch-up controls, but is not the only execution path. Any common runtime import requires explicit transpile-fixture inventory to preserve current compatibility.
