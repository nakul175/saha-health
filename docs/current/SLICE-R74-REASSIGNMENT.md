# Development slice r74 — automatic reassignment and queue-based assignment

3 October 2026 (IST). Built against the R11 source (slices 0a–11h and the independent anaemia-provider demonstration). Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. The reassignment order and queue rules are engineering defaults, not an approved care policy.

## What changed

- **Automatic reassignment of work that loses its owner (derived on read).** `lib/saha/reassignment.ts` computes, on every read, the open follow-ups, referrals and clinical safety cases whose named owner no longer holds the work — no owner, an inactive (suspended/removed) member, an owner who no longer has a care role, or one who no longer has normal care access to the patient. Each such item is shown as automatically reassigned to the responsible team queue (`Supervisors of …` for care tasks, `Clinicians of …` for safety cases) with the reason the owner was lost, the item's due date, urgency, source link and a recovery action. Nothing is copied, sent or notified: the reassignment is computed, not stored. This closes the "monitored queue" gap for unassigned work (BR-CARE-01) and makes every lost item discoverable and assignable without searching individual worker transcripts (BR-REP-01).
- **Monitored queue read.** `GET /api/reassignment` (`lib/saha/reassignment-server.ts`, `app/api/reassignment/route.ts`) is a viewer-scoped organisation read. Supervisors and owners see their teams; administrators are refused (the queue contains patient information) and a care worker without a care role is refused the reporting read, mirroring the exception queue.
- **Assignment directly from the queue.** The additive command `assignQueueItem` (`POST /api/commands`) names items rather than the patient: up to 20 targets, each a follow-up, referral (with its version) or safety case (with its version), a new owner and a reason of 10–500 characters. It is resolved to the patient on the server, audited, and the same authority is rechecked when an idempotent request is replayed. A steward (a supervisor, or an owner with a care role, in the patient's team — including a clinician holding a supervision delegation) may reassign any of the three kinds; a care worker may take ownership for themselves of an unowned follow-up or referral only. The new owner must be eligible: an active ASHA or clinician with normal access for care tasks, an active clinician with normal access for a safety case. Duplicate targets, stale referral or safety-case versions, closed items, already-owned items and ineligible owners are refused.
- **Safety-case reassignment beyond clinicians.** Because `assignQueueItem` treats a safety case as a queue item, a supervisor or owner of the patient's team can now reassign a safety case to a clinician directly from the queue — previously only a clinician could reassign one through Clinical governance. A care worker still cannot, and the new owner must still be an active clinician with normal access, so the clinical-owner rule is unchanged.

## Evidence

- 9 contract checks (`tests/reassignment-contract.mjs`): the derived-on-read queue lists a lost safety case (owner no longer a clinician), a lost follow-up (suspended owner) and an unowned referral with their responsible queues and reasons, while owned, completed, closed and other-team work is excluded; the read refuses administrators and care workers and scopes supervisors to their own teams; the command refuses non-schema input, short reasons, administrators, an owner outside the team, another team, duplicate targets, stale versions, closed items, ineligible owners and care workers assigning someone else or claiming an owned item; the command reassigns an unowned referral (referral version bumped, patient version unchanged, audit line "Reassigned …"), lets a care worker take ownership of an unowned follow-up ("Ownership taken …", patient version bumped), lets a supervisor reassign a safety case to a clinician ("Reassigned …"), combines a follow-up and referral in one command, and refuses reassigning to the current owner and missing/completed items.
- `node tests/action-plan-contract.mjs` (8 checks) and `node tests/exceptions-contract.mjs` (8 checks) still pass unchanged, and `tsc --noEmit` is clean, so the additive command and shared dispatch/schema edits do not alter existing behaviour.
- No HTTP/D1 or browser suite was run for this slice (the parallel-agent brief limits each agent to its own contract test).

## Limits

- The reassignment is a view plus an assignment command; there is no notification when work loses its owner (that remains demonstration-only elsewhere), and nothing is written until a steward or claimant runs the command.
- Protocol reviews and correction tasks are not assignable from this queue (they keep their existing team-queue / action-plan paths).
- The command loads organisation-wide (it names items, not a patient), so it uses the organisation-wide guard rather than the per-patient guard.
