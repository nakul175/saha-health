## Findings and bounded fixes

1. **Pending-plan impact omitted destination programme applicability.** An actual synthetic Worker/D1 workflow created a General care patient, a confirmed visit and queued submission, plus a second confirmed visit with a proposed plan. A draft retained the exact weight-to-kg mapping but restricted coverage to Maternal care. The queued submission was correctly counted as unmappable, while the proposed plan reported `pendingPlans: 1, changedPlans: 0`. The review assertion failed. `mappingImpact` now applies the same destination coverage check to pending plans, so this unusable plan is counted as changed. No stored plan, submission, manifest or approval is rewritten. The original observation is retained in [before evidence](../../tests/evidence/mapping-lifecycle-review/programme-impact-before.json), separately from the corrected [observation](../../tests/evidence/mapping-lifecycle-review/programme-impact-observed.json).
2. **Immutable hashes overflowed the phone history card.** Chromium at 390×844 measured a 304-pixel panel with 485 pixels of horizontal content. The review assertion failed. Wrapping is scoped to the reviewed mapping panel and preserves full source references, versions and hashes. Before geometry and the failed browser result remain separate from the corrected run.
