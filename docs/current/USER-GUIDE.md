# User guide

Saha is currently a private synthetic workspace. Use the assigned role and organisation; a visible button or owner title does not grant unrestricted patient access.

1. Sign in and check your organisation and team. If joining another organisation, give its owner the account code shown in your account and use the account-bound invitation. Site access must already allow you to open the application.
2. Open **People** or **My work**, select the intended person and verify context. Registration searches only permitted records. If identity is provisional, save draft information and seek clinician resolution through **Care handover** before consequential actions.
3. Start a visit. Enter observations with their date/time, source and units. Save shared facts and programme evidence. An unsaved form is held in browser memory; **Saved draft** means the server saved it. Missing observations should stay missing.
4. In **Review & act**, review the current person and facts and make the positive patient check. Confirmation freezes the record. Use a linked amendment for later corrections rather than attempting to edit confirmed history.
5. For assistance, review the task and disclosure, approve the exact request, inspect evidence and uncertainty, and select only the fields you want to apply. Application does not confirm the visit. A local parser result is rules-only, and an unable-to-assess result is not a clinical conclusion.
6. For submissions, review each destination and payload. Inspect each receipt separately. If one result is uncertain, use its recovery/lookup path and preserve its key; do not assume all destinations accepted.
7. For a referral, record an owner, due date and evidence for each supported transition. A recorded status is not automatic facility delivery. For reassignment, use **Care handover** so open tasks, referrals and responsibilities remain accountable.

## Interruptions and corrections

Resolve stale-version conflicts by comparing your edits with the current saved record. Do not switch patients with unsaved information. Ordinary workspace forms do not become durable offline storage simply because the browser remains open.

The separate **Field notebook** requires online preparation of a signed grant and supported Web Crypto. Protect its passphrase, lock it when leaving the device, and preserve encrypted pending drafts until sync has a durable server receipt. Do not clear storage to fix a conflict. Physical-device disconnected operation remains unqualified.

Identity corrections and wrong-person reports require a reason and retain original evidence. They cannot erase copies already sent to another system. Raise the appropriate care or support case when downstream correction is required.

**Clinical governance**, **Pilot readiness**, **Outreach** and **Expansion** currently support bounded local reviews, rehearsals or simulations. They do not send real reminders, start an approved clinical study or certify a provider. Seek the named clinical owner for concerns; technical support must not delay the established care escalation route.
