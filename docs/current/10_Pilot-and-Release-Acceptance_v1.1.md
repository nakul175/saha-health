## Measurement and decisions

Separate repeated entry, active task time, accepted destination actions, corrections, usable AI responses, referral outcomes and adoption. Preserve denominators, missingness, undated records, interruptions and exclusions. Do not interpret scoped request latency as end-to-end availability, worker activity as adoption, simulated receipts as real acceptance or a worker-entered referral completion as verified care outcome.

For a formal decision record: pin requirement/gate, build, content/model/connector versions, environment, protocol, population, raw evidence, measured result, limitations, defects, accountable reviewer, date and disposition. A changed cohort, dependency, membership or configuration may invalidate earlier in-app review. Reopen affected gates when evidence or intended scope changes.
