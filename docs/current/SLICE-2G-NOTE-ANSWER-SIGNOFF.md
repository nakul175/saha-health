# Development slice 2g — author and verification for notes and programme answers, and supervisor sign-off before sending

1 October 2026 (UTC). Builds on slices 2c and 2e. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged.

## What changed

- **Author for notes and programme answers.** The visit notes and each programme answer (an answer or an explicit absence reason, keyed by question id) now carry who recorded them: account id, display name, account code, role at the time and save time. The server sets the author from the authenticated identity whenever the note or answer changes, through the same after-command comparison slice 2e uses for measurements (`recordFactAuthors` in `lib/saha/fact-verification.ts`, rule in `stampEntryAuthors` in `lib/saha/fact-provenance.ts`). Unchanged entries keep their author, cleared ones lose it, a correction draft keeps the authors of copied entries, and clients cannot send an author (command-level `noteAuthor` and `answerAuthors` fields are dropped). Notes and answers saved before this slice, or unchanged since, show "Recorded by: not recorded".
- **Verify or dispute notes and answers.** A sibling of `verifyFact`, the patient command `verifyEntry` (`lib/saha/visit-signoff.ts`), lets a clinician verify or dispute the visit notes (`entry: "notes"`) or one programme answer (`entry: "answer:<question id>"`) of a current confirmed visit. The same rules as `verifyFact` apply: clinician role, the patient in the clinician's normal care scope (emergency access does not count), the visit's current record version, an entry that was recorded, a reason of at least 10 characters for a dispute, a different clinician from the one who recorded the entry, and a decision that differs from the current state. Reviews are appended to `entryReviews` (latest wins), each one writes a record revision and an audit event with the patient id, and the content and visit version do not change. Replays recheck role and scope under the current membership.
- **Owner sign-off policy.** `setSignOffPolicy` lets an organisation owner require (or stop requiring) supervisor sign-off before programme submissions are sent. The policy is per organisation, kept in the operations document with a version and an appended change list (time, owner, reason), and each change is audited. It is off by default.
- **Supervisor sign-off.** `signOffVisit` records a sign-off of a current confirmed visit by a supervisor of the patient's team, using the slice 1e helper (`supervises`), so a team-supervision delegate can sign off (the delegation id is kept on the sign-off and the use is audited) and, as in 1e, so can an owner with a care role in the team. The person who confirmed the visit, or who recorded any of its measurements, notes or answers, cannot sign it off. A sign-off is bound to the visit version and a SHA-256 of the visit's facts, programme answers, absence reasons and pinned packs; it is current only while all of them still match, so any later change, a correction or a void makes it stale. Sign-offs are appended (never removed), audited and written as record revisions; a second sign-off is refused while one is current. Replays recheck supervision under the current membership.
- **Checked before sending, on the server.** While the policy is on, `executePlan` (which sends demo destinations and queues HTTP destinations) is refused before any submission is created unless the visit has a current sign-off (`ensureSignOff`, run by the dispatcher). `sendSubmission` checks again inside `executeMis` on the state it then checkpoints before the programme request (`ensureSendSignOff`), so a missing or stale sign-off never authorizes a send, even for a delivery queued while the sign-off was current. The sign-off relied on is recorded on each submission created or sent.
- **Screens.** The visit review tab lists, below the visit notes, who recorded the notes and each programme answer and its verification state, with Verify and Dispute buttons for clinicians in scope. Confirmed visits show a sign-off box when the policy is on or a sign-off exists: the current sign-off, a stale earlier one, or "Not signed off yet", with a "Sign off for sending" form for supervisors of the patient's team and delegates. Each row in Programme submissions (both the full list and the paged list) shows "Supervisor sign-off: signed off by … (role), time" or "not signed off". Owners see the policy switch, with a required reason, in the Organisation view.
- **FHIR unchanged.** The FHIR document, the mock ABDM transfer and the FHIR example carry no note or answer authors, reviews or sign-offs; their output is byte-for-byte what slice 2e produced, so the pinned rule set `saha-fhir-r4-document-structure` 1.0.0 is untouched. Programme (MIS) payloads are also unchanged.

## Limits

- One author per note and per answer: whoever last saved a change to it. Changing only a fact-backed programme question's measurement credits the measurement (slice 2e), not the answer.
- Reviews of notes and answers are by clinicians only and do not block sign-off, plans or sending; a dispute is shown, not enforced. Reviews cannot be withdrawn to "unverified".
- The policy is per organisation, not per programme or destination, and covers every programme destination (demo and HTTP). EHR publication and ABDM transfer are not gated by it.
- A sign-off cannot be withdrawn; it stops counting only when the visit changes. A sign-off stays current if the supervisor later loses the role (the act is kept as recorded at the time); the send-time check does not re-evaluate the signer's authority.
- Under the 1e definition an owner with a care role in the team counts as a supervisor for sign-off; the confirmer and recorders are excluded, but an owner-clinician who did not touch the visit can sign it off.
- Sign-off is not shown in the FHIR outputs or programme payloads, and no coded provenance or verification status is emitted (the pinned FHIR rule set does not permit it).
- Owner policy changes are shown only as the current state in the Organisation view; the change list is kept in the operations document and the audit log, not displayed.

## Correction — 1 October 2026 (slice 6z)

On visits recorded before slices 2e and 2g there is no per-entry or per-measurement author, so the not-your-own checks compared against nothing: the confirmer could verify their own notes and the person who saved the programme answers could sign the visit off. Slice 6z treats the confirmer and, for answers, the answer saver as authors when none is recorded (and the confirmer for an unattributed measurement), and counts the answer saver as a recorder for sign-off. See [slice 6z](SLICE-6Z-SIXTH-REVIEW-FIXES.md), finding 4.
