# Development slice 6o — consent withdrawal and its downstream effects

30 September 2026 (IST). Stacked on the integrated round-1 build (slices 3g–6l). Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No migration.

## What changed

- **One withdrawal, every dependent item.** A care worker (ASHA or clinician with care access to the patient) can record a withdrawal of one permission (`withdrawConsent`: patient, patient version, purpose `ai`, `exchange` or `contact`, how it was received — in person, by phone or in writing — a reference of 3–200 characters and what the patient asked for, 10–1,000 characters). The permission must currently be given. `lib/saha/consent.ts` then stops or holds everything waiting on it and lists what already left Saha:
  - **External record sharing:** queued and retryable programme deliveries are cancelled with a delivery event (nothing was sent); deliveries that are sending or uncertain are listed as awaiting a receipt and are never resent under the withdrawn permission (the existing delivery check already refuses them); accepted deliveries are listed with their receipt as already sent and not recalled; unexecuted sharing plans are blocked; active EHR permissions (including expired ones still marked active) are revoked; prepared EHR publications are blocked, running or uncertain ones listed as awaiting a receipt, accepted ones listed as already sent; granted or requested ABDM consent artefacts (mock) are revoked, so further fetches under them are refused; linked care contexts and received ABDM records are listed as kept, with the reason. EHR identity links are not consent and are left alone.
  - **Optional AI assistance:** unsent assistant requests are stopped (nothing was sent); a returned output awaiting review is withdrawn from review and cannot be applied, and is listed as already sent unless the provider was the local rules assistant; a running request is listed and its output is withheld on return (the existing execution check); earlier AI results stay with the visit and are listed as kept; unexecuted plans are blocked because the permission version changes.
  - **Automated contact:** the contact preference's permission is turned off and its version increased; draft, approved and failed reminders are cancelled; simulated reminders are listed as already sent.
- **Consent history on the patient.** Every withdrawal, and every grant or withdrawal made with the existing permission switch or through contact preferences, appends an event to the patient's `consentHistory`: purpose, granted or withdrawn, time, account and display name, role, how it was received, reference, reason, the permission version before and after (the patient's permission revision for AI and sharing; the contact preference version for contact), and each downstream item with its kind, reference, state before and after, disposition (stopped, awaiting receipt, already sent, kept) and a sentence saying what happened. The same event is summarised in one audit row (counts per disposition, versions, channel and reference), and the patient change writes a record revision holding the full document.
- **The switch cannot skip it.** `setConsent` is now handled by the same module, with the same care-role check on first use and on replay. Withdrawing with the switch runs the same downstream stop and records the channel as "switch". Previously a switch withdrawal only blocked unexecuted plans; queued deliveries stayed queued (refused at send time) and unsent assistant requests stayed pending (refused at approval). Changing contact preferences records a contact grant or withdrawal when the permission value changes.
- **Patient screen.** A "Consent history" button opens the current state of each permission, a withdrawal form (for care roles, listing only permissions currently given) and the history with each item's disposition. A notice on the patient screen appears while any withdrawal left a delivery awaiting a receipt.
- Withdrawal is allowed during maintenance, like the switch withdrawal and cancellations. It is certified as a patient command (patient partition load); a contact withdrawal changes the organisation outreach document and so commits under the organisation guard.

- 85 HTTP/D1 assertions, requests and checks (`tests/consent-smoke.mjs`): with an HTTP programme connection onboarded in local D1 only, a demo destination, an EHR link and permission, an ABHA link, a linked care context, a granted consent with a completed mock fetch, a requested consent, an unsent local assistant request, contact preferences, a scheduled outreach item and a draft reminder: grants recorded by the switch and by contact preferences; refusals for administrator, supervisor, an unassigned ASHA, a stranger, schema and stale version; the sharing withdrawal's result counts, idempotent replay, a second withdrawal refused; each item's disposition and state; a send attempt reports the cancelled delivery; the mock fetch, a new EHR permission and a new plan refused; an AI withdrawal by another care-team clinician stops the request and approving it sends nothing; a contact withdrawal cancels the reminder and versions the preference; history order, three audit rows, three patient record revisions and the visit revision for the blocked plan. The AI part runs in a new organisation, because the shared test organisation's local assistant is placed under capability review by the governance suite.
- Changed expectations: `tests/mis-smoke.mjs` now expects the switch withdrawal to cancel the queued delivery (a send attempt reports "cancelled"; a manual cancellation is refused as already done), and `tests/agent-smoke.mjs` expects the pending request to be cancelled (approval returns "cancelled" with no call). `tests/outreach-contract.mjs` transpiles `consent.ts` and `abdm.ts` because `outreach.ts` imports the history helper; its count is unchanged.
- Browser check (headless Chromium, built Worker, synthetic owner): with Meena Devi's sharing permission given, one accepted demo delivery and one unexecuted plan, the "Consent history" dialog showed the switch grant; withdrawing sharing by phone with a reference recorded the event with the unexecuted plan "stopped" and the accepted delivery "already sent, not recalled"; axe-core found no WCAG A/AA violations in the dialog; no console errors. The accessibility audit (25 screens) reports none. `tests/evidence/slice-6o/`.

## Limits

- Nothing is recalled or erased outside Saha. Items already sent are listed for the team to tell the destination through its own process; there is no destination notification workflow in this slice (the rights-request destinations from slice 6b remain the tracked way to record that a destination was told).
- ABDM is the local mock. Revoking a mock artefact does not notify a consent manager, and linked care contexts cannot be unlinked in this build.
- Offline field copies on worker devices are not listed; they refresh at the next sync. Referral letters, printed documents and verbal disclosures are not tracked.
- Purpose-level only: there is no per-destination or per-data-class withdrawal, no scheduled expiry of the Saha permissions themselves, and no consent form or signature capture. The contact permission can also be changed through contact preferences, where the reference is the preference reference.
- The copy on the patient record keeps the latest 100 events and 100 items per event; the audit chain and record revisions keep the full trail.
- BR-SEC-03 and NFR-26 stay partial.
