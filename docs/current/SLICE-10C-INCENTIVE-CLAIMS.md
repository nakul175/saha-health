# Development slice 10c — worker incentive claims

2 October 2026 (IST), evening. Based on `main` at 150b9fc. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No migration. No payment, no real rate and no provider is involved.

## What changed

- **A synthetic incentive schedule the owner defines** (`setIncentiveSchedule`). A versioned list of placeholder amounts, one per kind of confirmed activity, in a synthetic unit (SYN): a confirmed visit, a confirmed visit accepted by a programme destination (the receipt is carried on the line), and an outreach item completed with a confirmed visit. Amounts are whole numbers from 1 to 1,000. Every change is a new version with a reason; earlier versions stay and every claim records the version it was priced on. A stale version number is refused (409). The schedule, every response, every claim view, the audit text and the CSV carry the notice that the figures are placeholders, are not an incentive rate of any government or programme, and that nothing is paid.
- **An ASHA prepares a claim from her own activity** (`prepareIncentiveClaim` for one of her teams and a period of at most 31 days that has started). Eligible lines are derived on the server from confirmed records, never typed in: visits she confirmed after her positive patient check that are still current, and outreach items she recorded as completed with a linked, current confirmed visit. Patients must be in her own care scope. A visit that evidences an outreach completion is claimed under the outreach line only, so one piece of work is one line. Each line names its source record, the visit that evidences it and the activity time. A claim holds at most 60 lines. The Care handover page previews the lines, the total and what was skipped (already claimed, kind not in the schedule) before she submits.
- **No line twice.** A visit can evidence one line and an outreach item can be on one line across every live claim (submitted, verified or approved). Returning or withdrawing a claim releases its lines. A retried or replayed command cannot claim the same source again.
- **The supervisor verifies, the owner approves** (`verifyIncentiveClaim`, `decideIncentiveClaim`). A supervisor of the claimant's team (slice 1e: team supervisor or an active supervision delegate; never an administrator) verifies the claim against the source records or returns it with a reason of at least 10 characters. Verification refuses a claim with a line whose evidence no longer stands (the visit was marked wrong-patient or corrected, or the outreach record changed) and says which. The owner then approves or returns it; approval re-checks the evidence. The claimant, the preparer and the verifier cannot verify or approve the same claim (segregation of duties). The claimant can withdraw her own claim while it is in review.
- **An approved claim is immutable and chained.** Approval stores the claim with a sequence number, the previous claim's digest, a content digest and a claim digest (`lib/saha/incentives.ts`, the slice 6j chain style: `SHA-256` of a fixed JSON array; the first claim links to 64 zeros). The content covers the claim, every line (source, evidence visit, one-way patient reference, amount, receipt), the total, the verifier and approver account codes and times. Any later command on an approved claim is refused (409). `verifyClaimChain` recomputes the whole chain and reports intact, empty or broken with the first break; the panel shows it. The hashing rule is published in the response and the CSV so an auditor can recompute without Saha.
- **Corrections are new linked claims.** `prepareIncentiveCorrection` (the claimant or a team supervisor) names lines of an approved claim and a reason for each; the new claim of type correction carries negative reversal lines linked to the original, goes through the same verification and approval, and is chained like any claim. The original is not touched. A line already reversed by a live correction cannot be reversed again; withdrawing or returning the correction releases it. A correction whose original is no longer approved cannot be approved.
- **CSV export of approved claims** (`GET /api/incentive-claims?format=csv`, owner or team supervisor). One row per line with the claim number, team, claimant and preparer account codes, period, schedule version, source reference, evidence visit, one-way patient reference, amount, receipt, reversal link, total, verifier and approver codes and times, chain sequence, previous digest, content digest and claim digest, then a manifest with the row count and the SHA-256 of the file body. Owners get every approved claim, supervisors those of their teams. The export carries no patient name, village or patient identifier, and negative amounts are written as numbers. Each export writes an access-audit event (slice 6t) before the file is returned, with its export identifier and digest.
- **Scope.** The ASHA sees her own claims, a supervisor those of the teams they supervise, an owner all claims. A line shows the patient's name only to a viewer whose own care scope includes the patient; an owner's authority does not widen that. Providers and administrators who are not owners are refused. Every transition writes an organisation audit event without patient content.
- **Storage.** The schedules and claims live in the organisation `operations` document (like delegations and indicator definitions), bounded to 50 schedule versions, 60 lines per claim and 500,000 bytes of claim state; at the limit new claims are refused and stored claims stay. No migration. Every command takes the organisation revision, so claims serialise like other organisation-wide changes.
- **Care handover page.** An "Incentive claims (synthetic)" panel shows the notice, the chain status, the schedule (an owner editor with placeholder amounts), the ASHA period preview and submit, and the claim list with verify, return, approve, withdraw and correction actions, each only where the server allows it, and the CSV download.

## Limits

- Synthetic and placeholder only. Saha holds no real incentive rate, no government or programme schedule, and no per-state or per-scheme catalogue. The three activity kinds are an engineering stand-in; which activities a real scheme pays for is not modelled.
- No payment, payment file, bank detail, disbursement, reconciliation against paid amounts or buyer report. "Approved" means recorded in Saha, not payable.
- Verification is a supervisor's check of Saha's own records (the same evidence matching as slice 4c). No beneficiary confirmation, field spot-check, counter-signature or independent audit exists.
- The chain is a tamper-evidence aid held in the same database. It is not an external anchor, a qualified electronic signature, a timestamp authority or a notarised record; someone with database write access who also recomputes every later digest is not stopped. Anchoring or signing outside the database is not built.
- Eligibility is the current rule set: visits the ASHA confirmed after her own patient check, and outreach items she completed with a linked visit. Follow-ups, referrals, immunisation sessions without a visit record, field-notebook drafts and activity for patients outside her scope are not lines. Patients since moved to another team follow the patient's current team, so a claim is prepared for a team the patient is in now.
- A claim holds at most 60 lines and covers at most 31 days; a busy worker prepares several claims. Stored claim state is capped at 500,000 bytes and 50 schedule versions, with no archive policy; the D1 row size limit is the real ceiling.
- Evidence can change after approval (a visit later marked wrong-patient). The approved claim stays as stored and the panel flags the line; a correction is the way to reverse it. Nothing reverses automatically.
- BR-CARE-08 stays partial. Real rates, beneficiary verification, payment and reconciliation, buyer reporting, external anchoring or signing and an archive policy remain.
