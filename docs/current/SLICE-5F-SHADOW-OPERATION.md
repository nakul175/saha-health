# Development slice 5f — shadow operation of a reviewed capability revision

30 September 2026 (IST). Stacked on slice 5e. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No migration.

## What changed

- **Starting a shadow (`startShadow`).** An organisation owner, or a clinician governance manager whose teams cover the capability, can put an independently reviewed capability revision that is not yet active into shadow, with a reason of 10–1,000 characters, until a date at most 30 days ahead. The scope is named teams within the manager's scope, or the whole organisation (owners only). Draft, active, suspended and retired revisions, revisions with any open issue (review, evaluation, owner or dates), stale versions and a second shadow of the same revision are refused. Administrators, supervisors, ASHAs and provider accounts cannot start, run or stop a shadow. Each shadow records the exact revision and manifest hash it covers. The start is audited with the statement that outputs are never shown to field workers, applied or added to action plans.
- **Running it (`runShadow`).** When a manager runs the shadow, the revision runs on up to 50 visits per command that were confirmed during the shadow period, are current (not voided or superseded), belong to a team in scope and have not been processed. It runs on the visit's recorded note with the same local input-quality checks and output validation as a visit request (slices 5c and earlier), and the output is stored with the per-field comparison in the governance record (`governance.shadows`). Nothing is written to the visit: no assistant request, no fact, no version change, no action plan item. The organisation snapshot sent to field workers does not include governance data. The run is audited with counts only.
- **What does not run.** A visit is recorded as not run, with its reason, when AI permission is not current (including a slice 6o withdrawal after the shadow started), processing is restricted (slice 6b), the person is outside the capability's age range, there is no recorded note, the capability's monthly budget check refuses (slice 5a), or the capability's provider is external: this build calls only the local rules provider in shadow, and live providers stay disabled by environment. A suspended, retired or changed revision does not run at all: the run is refused and the shadow is shown as halted (slice 5d). A run after the period's end, or after the shadow is stopped, is refused.
- **Comparison.** For each field, the shadow output is compared with the clinician-confirmed value on the visit: agreement (same number), disagreement, missing (a confirmed value and no shadow value), not recorded by the clinician (a shadow value and no confirmed value), and unable to assess (the shadow reported an explicit unable-to-assess outcome for a field that has a confirmed value). Fields with neither value are not counted.
- **Report.** The governance read (`GET /api/governance`) gives owners and clinician governance managers, for the capabilities in their scope, each shadow's state (in shadow, stopped, period ended, halted), period, scope, starter and reason, the visits processed (compared, unable, not run by reason), the number of confirmed visits waiting, and the counts per field. It contains no visit, person or value. The Clinical governance page shows this under each capability, with start, run and stop controls (`stopShadow` needs a reason).

## Limits

- A shadow runs when a manager runs it, 50 visits at a time; it does not run automatically when a visit is confirmed, and there is no schedule.
- Only the local rules provider is called in shadow. An external capability can be placed in shadow but each visit is recorded as not called; calling a live provider in shadow (with the checkpoint-before-call rule, cost reservations and identifier screening of the note) is not built.
- The comparison is against what the clinician confirmed, which is not independent ground truth; there is no adjudication of disagreements, no confidence interval, no per-language or per-group breakdown and no drift trend over time. The report is counts only and is not a clinical validation.
- Shadow runs use the visit's recorded note as input; visits without one are not run. Media, programme answers and other inputs are not used.
- BR-AI-04 and BR-AI-10 stay partial.

## Correction — 30 September 2026 (slice 6q)

A second independent review found that this slice's scope claims were wrong. Starting, running and stopping only checked that one of the manager's teams overlapped the capability's teams: a shadow's teams were not limited to the capability revision's reviewed teams (a whole-organisation shadow took every organisation team), and a clinician in one team could run or stop a shadow covering another team and process its patients' notes. Slice 6q limits shadow teams to the reviewed teams (organisation scope is the overlap), requires an owner or a member of every shadow team to run or stop (also on replay), and skips and counts visits outside the runner's normal care scope. See [SLICE-6Q-SECOND-REVIEW-FIXES.md](SLICE-6Q-SECOND-REVIEW-FIXES.md). The text above is kept as written.
