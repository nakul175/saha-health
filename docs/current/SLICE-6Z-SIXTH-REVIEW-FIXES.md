# Development slice 6z — fixes from a sixth independent review of slices 6u, 4f, 6v and 2g

1 October 2026 (UTC). Builds on `int7-6y-task-trace` (`b0ba193`: rounds 1–3 of the overnight build). Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No command, route or migration is added; the route count stays 48 and the certified patient command count stays 50. `abhaLinkReview` joins the access-audit action list.

These defects were found by a sixth independent review, carried out by an agent that did not write the code, of round-2 slices 6u (fourth-review fixes), 4f (pack installation), 6v (in-app alert delivery) and 2g (note and answer authors, verification and sign-off). That code had been built, tested and recorded as passing; the earlier runs did not test these cases. Each defect was reproduced on the base before it was fixed (the reviewer's scripts `a-proto.mjs`, `d-ack.mjs`, `e-orphan.mjs` and `b-legacy.mjs`, and the new regression checks), and the new checks fail on the base and pass after the fix. The slice 6u, 4f, 6v and 2g documents carry a dated correction; their original text is kept.

## What the review found, and the fix

1. **A question id named after an Object.prototype member made a required question optional (medium, slice 4f).** The manifest pattern accepted `constructor`, `toString`, `valueOf` and `hasOwnProperty`; `answers[q.id]` then read a built-in function, so a required question `constructor` counted as answered, `confirmVisit` confirmed without it, and an absence reason for it was refused (422) as if an answer existed.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/pack-installation.ts`, `lib/saha/programmes.ts` and the readers):* the manifest schema refuses every `Object.prototype` property name as a question id, show-when id, renamed-from id or rule question (`RESERVED_QUESTION_IDS`). Answers, absence reasons and answer authors are read through an own-property helper (`own`) wherever they are looked up by question id: the question list and unresolved check (`programmes.ts`), `saveProgrammeAnswers`, the reuse indicator, entry keys and authors (`fact-provenance.ts`, with a local copy of the helper so it keeps no catalogue import) and the two visit panels. This covers installed packs, compiled packs and any stored pack built without the schema.
2. **One owner's acknowledgement cleared other owners' notifications and blocked escalation (medium, slice 6v).** An organisation acknowledgement is recorded at the count its author saw, and an alert computed from a care report has a different count in each member's scope. Owner A acknowledged `destination-uncertain` at 3; owner B saw 5, unacknowledged, but B's notification was cleared and a high-severity occurrence open for 10 hours never escalated.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/alert-delivery.ts`, `lib/saha/alert-delivery-server.ts`):* chosen option — per-recipient clearing, one occurrence kept. The occurrence counts as acknowledged (all its notifications cleared, no escalation) only when every recipient's own view shows the acknowledgement. Otherwise the plan's new `clearFor` list clears the level-1 notification of each recipient whose own view shows it; the others stay active (or are restored), and the occurrence escalates on its severity threshold with reason `unacknowledged` and the detail "Not acknowledged in every owning member's own view within …" (no count). A partly acknowledged occurrence is not treated as re-opened. Splitting occurrences per recipient was not chosen: it would multiply escalations to the same owners for one organisation alert, and organisation-wide inputs (incidents, telemetry, budgets) give every recipient the same count, so for them nothing changes.
3. **Escalation notifications could be left on a closed, never-escalated occurrence (low, slice 6v).** The level-2 inserts were plain `INSERT … VALUES`, not tied to the guarded `UPDATE … WHERE escalated_at IS NULL AND closed_at IS NULL`; an evaluation on stale state that planned an escalation after another evaluation had closed the occurrence left active escalation rows.
   *Fix:* each escalation notification is `INSERT OR IGNORE … SELECT … FROM alert_occurrences o WHERE o.id=? AND o.closed_at IS NULL AND o.escalated_at=? AND o.escalation_reason=?`, after the update in the same batch: it is written only when this evaluation's guarded update escalated the occurrence. An escalation's update and inserts are never split across batches. The restore update is guarded the same way (`EXISTS` an open occurrence), so a stale evaluation never re-opens notifications of an occurrence closed meanwhile.
4. **On visits with no recorded author, the recorder could verify or sign off their own work (low, slice 2g).** Visits recorded before slices 2e and 2g have no per-entry or per-measurement author; the not-your-own checks compared against nothing, and `programmeCapture.savedBy` was not a recorder for sign-off.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/visit-signoff.ts`, `lib/saha/fact-verification.ts`):* when an entry has no recorded author, the visit's confirmer (`patientCheck.by`) and, for a programme answer, the person who saved the answers (`programmeCapture.savedBy`) are treated as its authors (`presumedEntryAuthors`); a measurement with no recorded author is treated as the confirmer's. The person who saved the programme answers is always a recorder for sign-off. Recorded authors are used unchanged where present.
5. **The 6u "neutral" ABHA refusal still revealed the number was held (low, slice 6u).** Its text and status (409, "cannot be linked here") occurred only when a record outside the requester's scope held the number.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/abdm.ts`, `lib/saha/server.ts`, `lib/saha/access-audit.ts`):* a gateway refusal of the one-time code and an out-of-scope duplicate now give the same refusal, `LINK_REFUSED` (422, "This ABHA could not be linked: the gateway did not accept this number and one-time code. Check both; if they are correct, ask an identity steward to review this record."). The out-of-scope case throws `AbhaHeldElsewhere`; the command path records an `abhaLinkReview` access-audit event on the holding record (visible only to members who may see that record, such as an identity steward), naming the requesting record, then returns the same refusal. Recording is best effort: if the event cannot be written, the requester still sees only the refusal. A duplicate inside the requester's scope keeps its specific message (they can see that record).
6. **Data exit exported every member's alert notifications (low, slices 6v and 6m).** Other supervisors' notifications carried counts from their own care scope and "care scope of …" escalation text.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/data-exit.ts`):* `alert_notifications` has a new `self` scope: only the exporter's own notification rows are exported. The rows left out are counted in the manifest file entry (`otherMembersRows`) and noted; they are not patient rows, so they do not count as withheld or affect completeness. Occurrence rows (alert ids, titles, times, escalation role and recipient count, no counts per scope) are still exported.

## Limits

- Per-recipient clearing means an owner whose view shows the acknowledgement can still receive the level-2 escalation for the same occurrence when another owner's view does not; the escalation says only that not every owning member's view showed it.
- An organisation acknowledgement still keeps one entry per alert: when owners with different counts acknowledge in turn, the latest replaces the earlier, so their views cannot all show it at once and the occurrence escalates on its threshold. Per-scope organisation acknowledgements were not added.
- The steward review is an audit event, not a work item: there is no identity-steward queue that lists it. The extra audit write makes the out-of-scope refusal slightly slower than a gateway refusal; timing was not equalised.
- On a pre-2g visit the confirmer is presumed to be the author of its notes, and the answer saver of its answers; a different person who actually wrote them cannot be identified, so the presumption can block a legitimate reviewer who confirmed the visit.
- Data exit leaves out other members' inboxes entirely; a buyer who needs every member's delivery history must export as each member or read occurrences only.
- A sign-off still stays valid after the signer loses supervisor authority (noted by the review; unchanged).
