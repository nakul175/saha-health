# Development slice 1e — scoped, time-limited delegation

30 September 2026 (IST). Stacked on slice 6o. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No migration.

## What changed

- **Two delegable authorities within the current role model** (`lib/saha/delegation.ts`):
  - *Team supervision.* A supervisor, or an owner with a care role, can delegate supervision of teams they belong to to a named active clinician who belongs to every one of those teams. It covers exactly the checks that previously required "a supervisor or owner of the patient's team": patient assignment within the delegated teams (`assignPatient`, both the patient's team and the destination), rights-request stewardship (assign, close, record destinations, lift a restriction), action-plan reassignment and referral stewardship (due-date changes and the escalation list).
  - *Reporting only.* An owner with a care role can delegate the scoped aggregate care report of teams they belong to to a named active clinician or supervisor. The delegate can open the Operations page and view and export (CSV) the aggregate report for those teams. A clinician delegate's report covers only the delegated teams; a supervisor's covers their own teams plus the delegated ones. It gives no patient records, exception queue, indicators, support export, telemetry, budgets or operations settings.
- **Grant, end and revoke.** `delegationGrant` names the kind, delegate, teams, end time (after now, at most 30 days away) and a reason of 10–500 characters. A member who already holds the authority, the grantor themselves, an ineligible role and a second delegation of the same kind for an overlapping team in force are refused; a member can hold at most five delegations in force. `delegationRevoke` (with version and reason) is open to the grantor, the delegate (giving it up) and any owner, and is allowed during maintenance. Delegations are stored with their full history in the organisation's operations document; none is deleted.
- **In force only while it is still true.** A delegation counts between its start and end, unrevoked, and only while the grantor still holds the authority for every delegated team and the delegate still qualifies. Suspending or re-roling the grantor, or moving the delegate out of the team, ends its effect immediately without another command.
- **Server-side enforcement.** One helper (`supervises`) replaces the three per-module "team steward" checks and is used by the assignment command; one helper (`reportingScope`) decides the report scope, the active-worker count (now by team list rather than the viewer's own team rows) and the CSV team list. Replayed commands run the same checks. Delegation never widens patient access: supervision requires the delegate's own team membership and the patient scope checks still run; reporting yields only aggregates.
- **Audit.** Grant and revocation are audited with names, teams, end time and reason. Each command that relied on a delegation adds a `delegatedAuthority` audit line naming the command, the delegation, its grantor and its end time. A CSV export under reporting delegation names the delegation in its export audit line.
- **Screens.** "Delegated authority" on the Organisation & team page lists the delegations the viewer granted or holds (owners see all), with state (in force, not in force, ended, revoked), scope text and a revoke control, and a form to delegate for 1, 7, 14 or 30 days. The workspace snapshot carries the viewer's delegations in force, so a supervision delegate gets the assignment controls and a reporting delegate gets the Operations page (without the exception queue).
- **Service accounts are not added.** Both identity providers accept only a signed-in person: the Sites dispatcher supplies a user identity, and the Cloudflare Access provider requires a subject and an email, so an Access service token is refused. There is no machine credential the Worker can verify, issue, rotate or revoke, and turning a worker's session into one would be exactly what BR-ID-09 forbids. Safe service identities need per-connector credentials issued and revoked outside the Worker (for example Access service tokens), an accountable owner and scope record, and audit naming both the initiating user and the executing service; that remains open. Connector credentials remain server secret bindings with per-organisation grants, as before.

## Limits

- Only the two authorities above can be delegated. Caseload transfers, outreach planning, identity-merge approvals, pilot roles, indicators, alert acknowledgement, membership management and every owner-only setting stay with the role. There is no programme, facility, geography or purpose dimension beyond teams.
- Delegations start when granted; there is no scheduled start, renewal or notification to the delegate, and an ended delegation is only shown as ended.
- Report-page reads are not audited per view (as before); CSV exports and commands are.
- The five-in-force and 30-day bounds are fixed in code, not organisation policy.
- No service accounts (see above). BR-ID-02 and BR-ID-09 stay partial.
