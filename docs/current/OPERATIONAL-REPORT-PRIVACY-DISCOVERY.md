## Full requirement scope

The original BRD source and complete paragraphs are pinned in `tests/evidence/operational-privacy-before/original-clauses.json`.

BR-REP-04 (Must, R1): “Reports shall enforce audience-specific aggregation and small-cell controls. Patient-level drilldown and exports shall require an approved purpose and separate access.” Acceptance: “Combining filters cannot reveal a suppressed small group or bypass person-level access restrictions.”

BR-REP-05 (Must, R1): “Exports shall identify report definition, period, filters, generation time and user, with protected access and expiry where appropriate. Official reporting shall pass a review and submission state distinct from a draft export.” Acceptance: “An exported report reconciles to the displayed governed measure and cannot be downloaded by an unauthorised role.”

This discovery concerns operational aggregate JSON/CSV protection and reporting-purpose enforcement. Patient-level export purpose/separate access, controlled download access/expiry and calendar-triggered reviewed official submission remain distinct buildable scope. Existing `aggregate-delivery.ts` already supplies independent prepare/approve/send/lookup and approval expiry; no duplicate approval workflow is proposed. Download access can expire; previously downloaded copies cannot be revoked. Formal audience/privacy policy, clinical measure approval, external provider acceptance and production qualification remain pending.

## Existing boundary and proposed bounded implementation

`operations-server.ts` builds an unrestricted `careReport`, derives alerts from it, audits and releases JSON or `exportCsv`. CSV retains current role/reach/delegation checks through `recordExportAudit`; JSON has its aggregate read audit. Neither format applies MIN_CELL or records a disclosure release, and neither explicitly enforces reporting purpose. `report-export.ts` version 1.1.0 labels exports as draft and reconciles raw detail/control totals. Merely renaming the file or suppressing its headline would leave alternate recovery channels.

Reuse the existing MIN_CELL=5, salted release candidates, population profiles, paired measure dependencies, complementary suppression and bounded CAS disclosure storage. A new leaf operational-disclosure projection should enumerate every patient-derived report field and its exact contributor/dependency semantics. At minimum protect cohort count, started/confirmed visits, daily/programme/destination breakdowns, open/overdue/undated work, reuse and agent activity as applicable, report-derived alerts, and every CSV control total. Unknown contributor support must withhold a measure rather than invent a clinical denominator. Distinct active-worker counts need their actual actor contributor semantics; they must not be relabelled as patient counts. Operator infrastructure/budget/support diagnostics are a separate nonclinical interface and must not be blanket-suppressed without a grounded dependency.

The displayed JSON projection and CSV must derive from one protected projection with explicit null/withheld states, never zeros substituted for withheld values. Control totals/checksums reconcile that projection; they must not reintroduce suppressed source numbers through detail, alerts or totals. Preserve exact window/filter/version metadata and the draft label. Map operational measures to existing indicator primitive identities only when their semantics exactly agree. For example current operational open follow-ups and the indicator's derived reopened-work semantics are not automatically interchangeable. Define truthful versioned operational dependencies where necessary.

Current release screening is per hashed receiver (`viewer:<id>` or `destination:<id>`). Copying that unchanged cannot fix the demonstrated cross-viewer subtraction. Propose a bounded organisation privacy-family bridge over recent compatible human aggregate releases, retaining per-viewer provenance and existing capacity limits, with a common salted population/dependency namespace. It changes disclosure screening, not each viewer's authorised clinical cohort. Historical legacy releases without sufficient contribution evidence must remain explicitly conservative/unknown; do not silently label them protected. Approval of this shared ledger interface is required before editing it. Cross-surface indicator/operational and cross-viewer tests are required; this is not a new approval mechanism.

Apply reporting purpose only to clinical report flows, retain current role/scope/delegation guards, reserve accepted disclosure evidence before return, audit protected views/refusals and re-read exact current role/purpose/reach/cohort/source after the final audit. Fail closed on changed basis or storage failure; do not replay clinical mutations or add public identity authority. A stale response may not release the old raw report or raw alert-derived values. Preserve authenticated infrastructure/support diagnostics under their existing operator guard.
