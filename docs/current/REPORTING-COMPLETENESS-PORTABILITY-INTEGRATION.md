## Stored evidence and privacy

An active owner also needs current normal reporting scope covering every member of every configured calendar team. Owner or administrator privilege cannot substitute for that scope. Exact immutable calendar policy JSON, proposal/independent review/release history, legacy and version-pinned source declarations, and fixed receipt outcome references are exported only as an indivisible calendar graph. Unsupported JSON, independent-history or receipt provenance is withheld whole rather than rewritten. Suspended or changed producers do not rewrite their historical declaration evidence or turn an old declaration into current completeness.

Completeness snapshot `data` is always omitted: it holds private original per-source timing and coverage sidecars. Only scoped snapshot id/time, opaque digest/basis and an included figure relationship are portable. A live snapshot without an attributable calendar is withheld; a figure-linked snapshot also needs the full included figure cohort and parent history. Manifest exclusions, table notes, scope text and README explicitly state that these hashes cannot reconstruct the omitted original body. Operational row scope being complete is not full archive fidelity. Existing protected per-person counting units, figure cells and disclosure ledgers remain withheld. No patient arrival count, band or raw source value was added.

Before export auditing, and after the audit commit, the server rechecks current owner/normal patient and whole-calendar reporting scope plus an exact private database basis for original calendar, source, operator membership/purpose and snapshot evidence. Source/evidence drift refuses the download. An audit committed before a final refusal remains recorded; refusal is not an assertion that no audit was written. This is a read/export path, not a retry or replay handler.

The leaked synthetic archive/log are retained under `retained-d1/normal-scope-before.*` and `whole-cohort-patient-scope-before.log`.
