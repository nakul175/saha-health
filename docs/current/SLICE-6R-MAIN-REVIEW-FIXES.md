# Development slice 6r — fixes from an independent review of slices on main

30 September 2026 (IST). Builds on slice 6q (second review fixes) at `1ae5cf7`. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No command, route or migration is added; route and certified patient command counts are unchanged.

These defects were found by a third independent review, carried out by an agent that did not write the code, in slices that had already been merged into GitHub `main` without independent human review (0a–3f, 4a, 6a–6h, 7a–7c). They had been built, tested and recorded as passing; the earlier passing runs did not test these cases. Each defect was reproduced on the base before it was fixed, with the reviewer's scripts (`household-leak.mjs`, `merge-bg.mjs`) or with the new regression checks, which fail on the base and pass after the fix. The slice 1, 1c, 6a, 6b, 6c, 7a and 7b documents carry a dated correction; their original text is kept.

## What the review found, and the fix

1. **Household sharing preview named patients outside the worker's scope (high, slice 1).** `householdView` named only members inside the viewer's scope, but the `shareable` preview it returned iterated every current household member, so `GET /api/household?recipient=` returned the name, id, relationship and open-item titles and due dates of a member the worker may not see whenever that member had granted the recipient a proxy.
   *Reproduction:* `household-leak.mjs` on the base: ASHA A (mother assigned to her) was shown `members: ['mother']`, `outsideScope: 1`, but `shareable` listed the daughter (assigned to ASHA B) with "TB sputum re-test" and its due date.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/household.ts`):* `shareable` takes an optional visibility predicate; the worker-facing view passes `allowed(viewer, person)`. Members failing it are neither named nor itemised, even with a grant, and are only counted (`omittedOutsideScope`). Without a predicate (the existing contract's direct use) the function behaves as before.
2. **Emergency-access restriction bypassed through indirect patient references (high, slice 6a with 1c and 6c).** `ensureExceptionalScope` resolved only `personId`, `visitId`, `submissionId`, `referralId` and `messageId`. A clinician whose only access to a patient was an emergency grant could propose a merge naming that patient as `survivorId` or `duplicateId`, and merge decide, unmerge, impact resolution (`caseId`) and legal-hold release (`holdId`) were not checked either.
   *Reproduction:* `merge-bg.mjs` on the base: `correctIdentity` under the grant was refused (403) but `identityMergePropose` naming the grant-only record returned a case id.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/exceptional.ts`):* `targets()` collects every patient a command names: `personId`, `personIds`, `survivorId`, `duplicateId`, `proxyPersonId`, `recipientId`, visits (`visitId`, `visitIds`), submissions, referrals, messages, identity cases and safety cases (`caseId`, both patients of an identity case) and legal holds (`holdId`). It fails closed: a command outside the urgent-care list is refused (403) when any named patient is reachable by the member only through an emergency grant.
3. **Patient history served unmasked ABHA numbers and received FHIR bundles (medium, slices 7a and 7b).** `GET /api/history` returned stored revision `data` and field `changes` as stored, including the full ABHA number (`abha.number`, ABHA history entries, consent artefacts, care contexts) and the full received bundles. `redactAbha` was applied only to snapshots.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/abdm.ts`, `lib/saha/server.ts`):* `redactRevision` is applied to every revision when history is served: person revision data goes through `redactAbha` (masked numbers, received bundles removed), `changes` entries for `abha.number` or `…abhaNumber` fields are masked, and `abdmReceived…` changes are replaced with "[received record withheld]". Stored revisions are not rewritten; redaction is on read.
4. **Reminder dispatch and care-context linking not paused by a processing restriction (medium, slice 6b with 7b).** `PAUSED_WHEN_RESTRICTED` lacked `messageSimulate` (the simulated reminder send) and `abdmCareContextLink`, so a reminder approved before a restriction still "sent".
   *Fix (`lib/saha/rights.ts`):* both commands are added, so they are refused (409) while a restriction is in force. When a restriction takes effect (restriction request closed as fulfilled), the patient's draft and approved reminders that have not been sent are set to `cancelled` with a reason; they are kept, the count is in the result (`cancelledMessages`) and the audit detail. A new reminder needs preparation and review after the restriction is lifted. Sent, failed and other patients' messages are untouched.
5. **Emergency-access review not limited by the reviewer's scope (low/medium, slice 6a).** `reviewer()` accepted any supervisor or care-role owner holding the patient's team id, regardless of whether that member may see the patient (for example an owner holding the ASHA role, who sees only assigned patients), and the review queue in `exceptionalView` listed patient names and grant reasons on that basis.
   *Fix (`lib/saha/exceptional.ts`):* `reviewer()` also requires `normallyAllowed(member, patient)`. The review command's authority check and the review and reviewed queues use it, so they no longer name patients outside the viewer's normal scope.

## Limits

- Received FHIR bundles remain inside the stored patient record and its revisions; they are withheld on read, not moved out of the patient JSON. Moving them to separate storage is not built.
- The household fix covers the worker-facing view. `shareable` called without a predicate still considers every member; the only such caller is the contract test.
- Emergency-access references are resolved from what the command's partition loaded. A legal hold or case naming a patient who is not loaded for that command is not checked by this guard; retention and merge commands still need their own authority (owner; two stewards with normal access).
- Reminder cancellation happens only when a restriction takes effect through `rightsClose`; outreach state is changed only when it is loaded for that command. The send refusal applies regardless.
- The review scope rule means an owner who holds the ASHA role cannot review emergency access for patients not assigned to her; a supervisor or care-role owner with normal scope must.
- These fixes have not been independently reviewed. BR-ID-07, BR-SEC-11, BR-SEC-10, BR-INT-14 and BR-ID-08 stay partial.
