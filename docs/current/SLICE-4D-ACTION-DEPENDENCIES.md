# Development slice 4d — action dependencies and referrals with several sources

30 September 2026 (IST). Part of slice 4 of the development-completion plan (NEXT-DELIVERY row 4). Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. Which work should wait for which is a clinical decision recorded by staff, not an approved care policy.

## What changed

- **Dependencies between plan items.** `linkPlanItems` (patient, patient version, dependent item, blocker item, a reason of 10–500 characters) records that one open programme follow-up or referral waits on another open follow-up, referral or protocol review of the same patient. A clinician with normal (not emergency) access to the patient, or a supervisor, owner or supervision delegate of the patient's team (slice 1e helper), may record it; administrators, owners outside the team, ASHAs and other teams are refused. The same check runs when an idempotent request is replayed.
- **Refusals.** An item cannot wait on itself; both items must belong to the named patient (a follow-up, referral or protocol evaluation of another patient is refused without saying whose it is); both must be open; a protocol review is never held back; two follow-ups shown as one reminder cannot wait on each other; a pair already recorded is refused; and a link that would close a cycle, directly or through other waiting items, is refused. A patient can hold at most 50 active and 200 recorded dependencies. Merged records refuse new dependencies.
- **Removal.** `unlinkPlanItems` (patient, patient version, dependency, a reason of 10–500 characters) by the same people marks the dependency removed with who, when and why. Nothing is deleted: the patient keeps every dependency, including removed ones, and both commands write an audit line naming the two items and the reason. A removed pair can be recorded again.
- **Plan effect.** While the blocker is open work in the plan, the dependent item shows as "Waiting for …" with the blocker's title, status, attention and days overdue. It is not counted as overdue and is listed after all other work. An ownership gap still escalates a waiting item, because it needs an owner either way. The blocker keeps its own place, so an overdue or escalated blocker still surfaces first, and it lists what it holds back. When the blocker is completed, declined, cancelled or reviewed (or its visit is corrected), the dependency reads "Blocker closed" and the item returns to its own due-date order. A chain (A waits on B, B waits on C) shows B as waiting in A's line. The plan response lists every dependency with its state (waiting, blocker closed, item closed, removed).
- **No automatic dependencies.** Decision: none are derived. Programme follow-ups and referrals carry no structured link to each other (a follow-up comes from a programme episode, a referral from a visit), so inferring "this follow-up waits on that referral" would mean guessing from titles or dates. Staff record each dependency with a reason instead. If packs later declare "after referral" follow-ups, a derived dependency can be added from that declaration.
- **Referrals with several sources.** `linkReferralSource` (referral, referral version, source kind, source id, a note of 5–500 characters) links one more visit, protocol evaluation or programme episode of the same patient to an open referral. Each link keeps its kind, the source's own time (visit confirmation, evaluation time or episode creation), when it was linked, by whom and the note. The referral's own visit stays its first source; it, a voided or corrected visit, a duplicate link, another patient's record and closed referrals are refused, and a referral holds at most 20 links. The referral owner with current access, a clinician with normal access, or a steward of the patient's team may link; the check is repeated on replay. The referral version increases and an audit line names the source. Existing single-source referrals are unchanged.
- **Views.** Referral cards list every source with its kind and time and offer "Link another source". The action plan lists every source of a referral, a "Wait on another item" form on follow-ups and referrals for clinicians and stewards, the waiting and holds-back lines, and a Dependencies list with a remove form that needs a reason.
- **Partition.** `linkPlanItems` and `unlinkPlanItems` name one patient and write only that patient's row; `linkReferralSource` names one referral and writes only that referral's row. All three are certified patient commands (48 in total).

## Limits

- Dependencies change only the action plan. The referral escalation queue (`GET /api/referral-escalations`), caseload and handover overdue counts, and reminders still use each item's own due date.
- Only follow-ups and referrals can wait; safety cases, outreach work and assistant disagreements neither wait nor block. A protocol review can block but never waits.
- When two follow-ups are shown as one reminder, a dependency of either makes the combined line wait.
- Sources are linked after a referral exists; creating one referral from several packs at once, and programme pack migration and installation, are not built. Linked sources cannot be unlinked; a wrong link is corrected by a note on a later link or by closing the referral.
