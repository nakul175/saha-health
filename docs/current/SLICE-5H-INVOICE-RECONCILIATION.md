# Development slice 5h — AI invoice reconciliation

30 September 2026 (IST). Stacked on the integrated round-3 build (slices up to 2f). Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No migration and no new route.

## What changed

- **Recording an invoice summary.** Until now AI spend was only an estimate from recorded cost rates and token use (slice 5a). An organisation owner can now record a provider's invoice summary with `opsInvoiceReconcile`: a CSV of up to 12 lines and 4,000 characters with the header `provider, model, month, input_tokens, output_tokens, amount_usd, invoice_reference` (any order, each once; RFC 4180 quoting), a flag threshold of 0.1–100% and a reason of 5–500 characters. Every line must name the same provider, month and invoice reference, each model once; the month is `YYYY-MM` from 2020-01 and not in the future; token counts are whole numbers; the amount has at most 6 decimals. The invoice reference is a short reference string (letters, digits and `. _ / # : -`); no invoice file is uploaded or stored. The CSV travels only in the command body, never in a URL. Any problem refuses the whole file (422) with the row numbers and reasons, and nothing is stored.
- **What it is compared with.** For that provider and month, Saha's own records of agent requests that reached the provider (sent calls; unsent, local-rules and other-month requests excluded), per model: requests, recorded input and output tokens, requests without recorded usage, and an estimated USD amount at the current cost assumptions, with a sent request without recorded usage counted at the slice 5a reserved allowance (6,000 tokens at the higher rate). Models on the invoice without Saha records, and models Saha recorded that the invoice does not show, are listed too.
- **Differences and flags.** Each line shows the invoiced minus estimated tokens and USD, and each difference as a percentage of the invoiced figure. A line is flagged when the token or USD difference exceeds the owner's threshold (strictly greater), when the invoice shows none while Saha recorded some, or when no cost rates are recorded (the USD side cannot be estimated).
- **Append-only and audited.** Each reconciliation is kept in the operations record with a snapshot of both sides, the cost assumptions used, the threshold, the owner and the reason. A later reconciliation for the same provider and month names the one it supersedes; neither is changed. At most 60 are kept; beyond that a new one is refused rather than an old one pruned. The audit line records the reference, provider, month, invoiced and estimated totals, the number of lines above the threshold, any superseded record, and that budgets and cost assumptions were unchanged. Nothing changes budgets, rates or requests. Administrators and supervisors are refused (403), including on replay; the command is available during maintenance like other operations commands.
- **Operations page.** The Service & incidents tab shows "AI invoice reconciliation" to owners and administrators, the same people who see budgets (slice 5a); supervisors receive nothing. Owners paste the CSV, set the threshold and give a reason. Each record lists the per-model lines with the flag reason, and is marked current or superseded.
- **Alerts (rules 1.2.0).** Two owner-owned alerts for the previous UTC month (slice 6g/6h mechanism; acknowledged only by an owner): *AI invoices that differ from Saha's estimates beyond the owner's threshold* (high; count of current, not superseded, reconciliations with a flagged line, each named with its provider, reference and number of flagged lines) and *AI providers used without a recorded invoice reconciliation* (normal; count of providers with sent requests that month and no reconciliation). The current month is shown in the table but never alerts, because it is not complete.

## Decisions

- One reconciliation covers one provider and one month, because an invoice does. Supersession is keyed on provider and month.
- Estimated USD uses the cost assumptions recorded at reconciliation time, not historical rates; the snapshot keeps the rates used so a later rate change does not alter an earlier record.
- The flag is relative to the invoiced figure; a difference exactly at the threshold is not flagged.
- Alerts consider the previous month only, so a completed month that was never reconciled, or whose current reconciliation is flagged, stays visible until an owner records one.

## Limits

- The invoice figures are what an owner typed or pasted; there is no provider billing API, no invoice file, no currency other than USD and no check that the reference exists.
- Saha's side covers agent requests only. Evaluations, shadow runs, legacy free-text AI, hosting, storage, MIS and EHR costs are not counted, so an invoice that includes them will differ.
- Rates are organisation-wide and current; there are no per-provider, per-model or historical rates, so a month reconciled after a rate change is estimated at the new rates (the record keeps the rates it used).
- Nothing is delivered: alerts appear when the Operations page is opened. Reconciling never changes a budget, a rate or a request; an owner does that separately with a reason.
- HTTP tests cannot create sent external requests (live providers are disabled in the sandbox), so Saha-side estimates, the unreconciled-month alert and reserved-allowance lines are covered by the contract suite.
- BR-AG-07, BR-AI-12 and BR-OPS-04 stay partial.
