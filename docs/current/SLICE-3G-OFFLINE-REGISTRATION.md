# Development slice 3g — offline registration with ordered sync

30 September 2026 (IST). Builds on slices 3a–3f. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged.

## What changed

- **One command for a new person.** `registerFieldPerson` creates a person, an optional first draft visit and an optional referral request in one commit, so they exist together or not at all. The person is assigned to the worker who registered them, in a team the worker belongs to (ASHA or clinician only). The notebook's registration id and capture time are stored on the person (`fieldRegistration`) and the visit (`fieldRegistrationId`).
- **No second person on resend.** A send with the same operation key replays as before. A send with a new key but a registration id already on a person returns that person and visit (`alreadyRegistered: true`) and records an audit line; nothing new is created. Another worker cannot reuse the id, and a worker who has since lost access to that person is refused with 409.
- **Duplicate review is the existing one.** The command calls `reviewRegistration` (slice 1 identity rules): possible matches in the worker's permitted records refuse with 409 until the worker reviews them; the review carries the hash from `POST /api/patient-matches`; a stale or wrong hash is refused. A provisional registration keeps its draft but cannot be confirmed until a clinician resolves identity.
- **A referral request is not a referral.** It is stored on the draft visit (`referralRequest`: facility, reason, who, when, capture time). No referral record is created and no facility is contacted. A request needs the first visit.
- **Register offline in the notebook.** "Register a new person" in the field notebook works without a connection: name, age, sex, village, programme, and a care team from the organisation and teams saved at the last online workspace load; optionally first-visit facts (the same fields as a visit draft) and a referral request. The notebook applies the server's limits before queueing. At most 10 registrations wait on the device. Registrations are stored inside the encrypted notebook.
- **Sync all, in order.** "Sync all" sends queued registrations first, then visit drafts for existing patients, then queued photos and voice notes (reusing `sync()` and `uploadMedia()`). An uncertain outcome stops the run, so nothing later goes ahead of it; refusals are routed to review and the run continues.
- **Uncertainty and refusal.** A registration keeps its operation key while its outcome is uncertain (network failure, 5xx, maintenance, a 2xx that does not name the person); a retry sends the same key and body. A refusal (400, 403, 404, 409, 410, 413, 415, 422) moves it to review with the server's reason. For a 409 the notebook fetches the possible matches: with candidates it shows them and asks the worker to choose "a different person" or "not sure (provisional)" with a reason of at least 10 characters, then resends with the review under a new key; "same person" discards the registration. Too many matches, or a conflict with no visible candidate, stay in review. Nothing in review is resent until the worker acts.
- The Hindi strings added here have not been reviewed by a native speaker.

## Evidence

- 51 HTTP/D1 assertions (`tests/field-registration-smoke.mjs`): a registration without a visit; person, draft visit and referral request created together with an audit line and no referral record; replay with the same key; a new key and even changed details returning the same person and visit; another worker's reuse of the id refused; a possible duplicate refused, the match search returning the earlier person, wrong-hash and short-reason reviews refused, a distinct review creating a second record with the review hash, a stale review refused and a provisional review creating a draft that cannot be confirmed; administrator, supervisor, outsider, other-team and unknown-team refusals; referral request without a visit, future capture time, future observation time, half blood pressure and a malformed id refused with nothing created; a replay refused after the worker's role changed.
- 65 client assertions (`tests/field-registration-client.mjs`), with real Web Crypto and the real sealing functions, injected storage and network: an older notebook opens with an empty queue; teams offered only from the worker's own teams; twelve local refusals with nothing queued or sent; names not present in the stored notebook; survival across lock and unlock; Sync all order (registrations, visit draft, media) with a lost registration response stopping the run and the retry using the same key and body; candidates shown and not resent until reviewed; the reviewed resend under a new key; a refusal resent only on request, with a new key and the same registration id; a new-key resend after an unseen success returning the same person; temporary failure and a 2xx without a person kept pending; overflow and candidate-free conflicts routed to review; every body valid for the server's own schema (transpiled from `lib/saha/field-registration.ts`); discard, removal, the 10-item limit, and another account refused.
- Browser check (headless Chromium, real IndexedDB, built Worker, synthetic ASHA): prepared a visit online, went offline, edited the draft, registered a possible duplicate and a new person with a first visit and referral request; no request left the browser while offline. Online, Sync all sent the two registrations first (the duplicate was refused and its candidates fetched), then the visit draft; the worker chose "a different person" with a reason and the registration synced. The server then held two people of that name (one registered from the field), the new person with a draft visit carrying the referral request, and the edited draft. No console errors. `tests/evidence/slice-3g/offline-registration-browser.json`.
- All suites: 1,551 HTTP/D1, 895 contract/checkpoint (830 plus 65) and 21 restore assertions.

## Limits

- Registration and sync are tested with injected networks and one headless browser, not on physical devices, with real intermittent networks or background sync. Nothing syncs until the worker opens the notebook and presses a sync button.
- The team list is whatever was saved at the last online load; a team change since then is caught only by the server at sync.
- Duplicate search covers only records the worker may see (an ASHA sees only their assigned patients), as in the online registration. A registration may still duplicate a person outside that scope.
- A referral request waits for a clinician; turning it into a referral is not built in this slice. Programme questions are not asked for a newly registered person (they have no enrolment yet).
- Registered people are not added to the offline caseload for further offline visits until the worker loads the workspace and prepares a visit.
