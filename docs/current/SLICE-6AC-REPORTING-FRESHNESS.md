# Development slice 6ac — source freshness and late arrivals in indicator results

2 October 2026 (UTC). Builds on `int9-7g-aggregate-delivery` (`49ab4be`: rounds 1–5 of the overnight build) and on slices 6d (indicators), 6s and 6w (registry and differencing ledger), 6aa (population differencing check), 3g (offline registration) and 3j (notebook capacity). Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged. No AI model is involved. No route, command or migration is added: the route count stays 49 and the certified patient command count stays 52. The differencing check, the release format and the indicator CSV are unchanged.

## What changed

- **Source freshness per team.** An indicator result (`POST /api/indicators`) now carries `freshness`: for each team the viewer belongs to (or the one team the query names) and for each of four sources, the latest record time, how many records arrived in the query window and how many of those arrived late. The sources are online visits (arrival: confirmation; described time: the visit date entered by the worker), field-notebook syncs (arrival: the sync; described time: the visit date entered offline), imports (arrival: the import batch; described time: an imported enrolment's start date — imported patients and identifiers carry no source time and are never counted as late) and programme receipts (arrival: acceptance with a receipt; described time: when the submission was queued). A record is late when it arrived more than 72 hours after the time it describes. Visit dates are local date-times without a zone and are read as UTC; the 72 hours absorb that difference. Voided and superseded visits and unconfirmed online drafts are not records.
- **No new differencing channel.** Freshness is computed over the viewer's own teams only, from the viewer-scoped load; the query's village and programme filters are never applied to it, so the figures do not change with those filters and a team the viewer does not belong to is never reported. Every count is a band, never an exact number: "fewer than 5" (0 to 4, so neither zero nor a small count can be told apart), "5–19", "20–99", "100 or more". The late share is stated to the nearest 10 percentage points and only when at least 20 records in the window have a described time and neither the late nor the on-time records number 1 to 4; otherwise it says why it is not stated. The latest record time is given to the hour. None of these figures are recorded in the differencing ledger or included in a released cell, and they are not in the CSV export.
- **Late arrivals since the previous release.** Each released result (not a withheld one) carries `lateArrivals`. For an indicator whose numerator or denominator is built on visits or referrals, it states when the viewer was last given the same figure (same series and filters, any window, from the seven-day ledger as it stood before this query was recorded) and how many late visits were confirmed since then and now count in this result's window and definition cohort, as a band. An indicator with no visit-based source says late arrivals do not change it; a figure with no earlier release says so. Referral-based indicators count the late visits referrals are created from, not the referrals.
- **Data still syncing.** When a team's patients carry field grants that were prepared more than 12 hours ago and are neither synced nor revoked, the team's freshness carries a warning ("Data still syncing: … (fewer than 5 field grants). Results for this team may rise when it arrives."), with the number of grants as a band.
- **Field sync time.** `syncFieldVisit` now stamps the visit with `fieldSync: {leaseId, at}` so a synced field visit keeps the time it arrived after its grant is pruned. Visits synced before this slice have no stamp and are not counted as field syncs or as online visits until they are confirmed (then as online visits). Offline registrations (slice 3g) already record their sync time on the person and are counted from it.
- **Workspace.** The Operations page's Indicators section shows a "Source freshness" table per team (latest record, arrived in window, late, late share with the reason when not stated) with the syncing warning above it, and a line under each released result stating the late visits since the viewer's previous release.

## Limits

- "Late" is a single 72-hour threshold for every source; it is not configurable per programme and does not follow any programme's reporting calendar.
- The described time is what the worker or source entered; a wrong visit date makes a record look late or on time. Visit dates have no time zone.
- Late arrivals are counted for visit-based indicators only; follow-ups, enrolments, identity reviews and submissions changing state after a release are not counted as late arrivals. Referral indicators count the late visits behind them.
- "Since the previous release" uses the seven-day differencing ledger; an earlier release is not known. It does not show how much the figure changed, only how many late visits now count in it.
- Banding protects exact counts but still allows a range to be inferred; a viewer who can open every record in their team (as supervisors can) learns nothing beyond what those records show.
- Field visits synced before this slice carry no sync time. Work that was never prepared as a grant (for example a registration still only on a device) cannot be seen by the server and raises no warning.
- BR-REP-03 stays partial: the figures are shown beside indicator results only, not on aggregate reports (slice 7g) or exports, and nothing marks a released figure as affected after the fact.
