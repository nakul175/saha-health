# Development slice r08 — facility and geography/locality dimensions in the access policy

3 October 2026 (IST). Builds on slices 6t/10h (access audit), 1/1b/1f (households and caseload transfer) and 6n (environments). Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged.

## What changed

- **The need.** `lib/saha/access.ts` constrains care scope by organisation, role, team membership and direct ASHA assignment only. BR-ID-02 requires access to be constrained by facility and geography/locality too, BR-OPS-01 requires administrators to configure those dimensions, and BR-ID-04's locality transfer remains open. This slice adds the two dimensions and the owner-managed configuration, while defaulting to the existing team + assignment scope when no policy is configured, so nothing existing changes.
- **Model.** An owner defines *localities* (named geographies) and *facilities* (named points of care; a facility may sit in one locality), then attaches a facility and/or a locality to teams and, as an override, to individual members. The configuration is one owner-managed, version-checked and audited document, `operations.accessScope` (`lib/saha/facility-scope.ts`), like delegations and the indicator registry — no migration.
- **Commands** (owner-only, each version-checked and audited): `addLocality`, `addFacility` (with an optional locality), `setTeamLocation`, `setMemberLocation` (facility/locality, `null` clears), and `setAccessScope` (`enforceFacility`/`enforceLocality`). `setAccessScope` refuses to turn a dimension on until at least one facility/locality is defined. The command schema is added to `lib/saha/platform.ts` and dispatched in `lib/saha/server.ts` (`applyFacilityScope`/`authorizeFacilityScope`).
- **Scope predicate.** `lib/saha/access.ts`'s `allowed(m, p, now?, scope?)` takes an optional access scope. With no scope, or with both dimensions off, it is exactly the previous team + assignment check. When a dimension is enforced, a member is additionally limited to patients whose `facilityId` (or `localityId`, resolved through the facility for locality) matches the facilities/localities attached to their teams and their own assignment; a member with no facility scope under an enforced dimension, and a patient with no matching facility/locality, are denied (fail closed). Emergency-access grants still bypass the restriction, as before. The authoritative gate in `lib/saha/store.ts` (`scope`) passes the policy, so the workspace snapshot, every command and patient detail read enforce it.
- **Patient location.** `Person` gains optional `facilityId`/`localityId`; the existing `assignPatient` command accepts optional `facilityId`/`localityId` (validated against the configured facilities/localities) to set or clear a patient's location.
- **Read.** `GET /api/facility-scope` (`lib/saha/facility-scope-server.ts`, `app/api/facility-scope/route.ts`, route name registered in `lib/saha/monitoring.ts`) returns the full configuration to owners (facilities, localities, team and member assignments with resolved names) and only the version to other active members.

## Evidence

- 38 contract checks (`tests/facility-scope-contract.mjs`): the no-policy and configured-but-off paths reproduce the existing team + assignment scope; owner commands add localities/facilities, attach a team and enforce facility scope; a member in a scoped team sees a patient in their facility and is denied a patient in another facility or with no facility; a member-level override widens one member without changing the team; locality enforcement resolves a patient's locality through their facility and denies a different locality; emergency access still bypasses the restriction; non-owners are refused before any change; a stale version is refused (409); every mutation bumps the version exactly once and appends an audit event; duplicate names, unknown references and enabling a dimension with no facility are refused with clear errors; the read view is owner-only; `scopeAllows` is a pure, additive predicate.
- `node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit` reports no errors in the slice's files (`facility-scope.ts`, `facility-scope-server.ts`, `access.ts`, `store.ts`, `platform.ts`, `operations.ts`, `monitoring.ts`, `app/api/facility-scope/route.ts`). The full-project run still reports unrelated errors from other in-flight slices in the shared working tree.
- Reports in `tests/evidence/r08/`.

## Limits

- **Read-path threading is partial.** The authoritative gate (`store.ts` `scope`) is wired, so the workspace snapshot, commands and patient detail are correctly constrained. The SQL pre-filters (`viewerScope`) and the per-row re-checks in a few auxiliary read routes (`patientPage`, worklists, the exception queue, household previews, the responsibility-gap view) still apply team scope only until those call sites pass the policy; a follow-on should thread the access scope through them, exactly as `scope` was threaded here.
- **Locality transfer of caseloads is not implemented.** BR-ID-04's "locality transfer" (bulk caseload transfer keyed by locality) is a follow-on; the existing slice 1b/1f bulk transfer remains team/keyed and unchanged.
- **No migration.** The configuration and the patient `facilityId`/`localityId` live in JSON (`operations.accessScope` and the patient document), so there is no new table or SQL migration; this slice does not alter `db/schema.ts` or `drizzle/`.
- Facility and locality names are free text, not a federated directory; matching is by the organisation's own identifiers only.
