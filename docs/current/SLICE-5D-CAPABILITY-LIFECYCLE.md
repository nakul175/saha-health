# Development slice 5d — capability lifecycle, replacement and in-progress work

30 September 2026 (IST). Stacked on slice 5c. Synthetic data only; formal acceptance unchanged.

## What changed

- **Lifecycle stages (BR-AI-04).** Each governance package now shows a stage in the BRD vocabulary, derived on read from what is already recorded (`lib/saha/capability-lifecycle.ts`): *registered* (a draft; a capability still needs a passing version-bound synthetic evaluation), *technically verified* (a draft capability with a passing evaluation bound to its exact manifest), *approved for its scope* (independently reviewed, awaiting owner activation), *active*, *suspended*, *retired* (withdrawn without a replacement) and *replaced*. The Clinical governance page shows the stage and what it means next to each package.
- **Replacement.** When an owner activates a reviewed revision, the revision it retires now records `replacedBy`. Older retired revisions without that field are matched through the replacement's history (a later revision of the same key that was made active), so they also show as replaced, with the replacing revision number. A withdrawn revision is shown as replaced once a later revision is activated.
- **Which runs used which version.** Assistant requests already stored the exact package id, version and manifest hash they were prepared under. Governance managers now see, for each capability revision, how many requests used exactly that revision, by status, and when it was last used. These are counts only.
- **In-progress work.** Once a capability revision can no longer be used (suspended, retired, replaced or failing its checks), the work begun under it is identified with the action it needs: an unsent request to cancel, a sent request whose result will be withheld, a result awaiting a decision that can no longer be applied (review or dismiss it), or values applied to a visit that is still a draft (check them before confirming). Confirmed visits keep their history and are corrected through the usual path. The Clinical governance page shows these counts for the viewer's patient scope and the action on each related record; suspending or retiring a capability returns the organisation-wide counts and writes them into the audit line, and a safety hold on a capability adds them to the safety case audit line.
- **Unchanged rules, now tested as lifecycle.** Only an owner activates, after independent review by a different clinician and a passing evaluation of the exact manifest; a provider account cannot activate anywhere (slice 5b). Suspension, retirement or replacement refuses new requests at preparation and at sending, and a result obtained under a revision that is no longer current cannot be applied.

## Evidence

- 25 contract assertions (`tests/capability-lifecycle-contract.mjs`): the four stages to activation; activation refused for the package author, a provider account and a provider outside a sandbox; runs bound to the exact version; use counts by status; the impact queue with each action and no action for a confirmed visit; replacement recorded and shown, including for a record without `replacedBy`; applying a result from the replaced revision refused; cancelling and recording a decision take items off the queue; suspension returns and audits the counts and refuses new requests and application; retirement without replacement, then replaced by a later revision; a safety hold records the counts.
- 89 HTTP/D1 assertions (`tests/capability-lifecycle-smoke.mjs`, including 34 evaluation preparation and case commands), in a fresh organisation with a local-rules capability and two 16-case synthetic evaluations: the stages through activation, a non-owner clinician refused activation, use counts, replacement by a second revision with the first revision's sent and unsent requests identified, sending and applying refused, cancellation clearing the queue, and suspension of the second revision returning and auditing its in-progress work and refusing new requests.
- Browser check (headless Chromium, built Worker, synthetic owner and reviewer): the AI capabilities tab showed revision 1 as "Replaced. Replaced by revision 2." with "Assistant requests on this exact version: 2 (1 review, 1 pending)" and "In-progress work in your scope: 1 unsent request to cancel, 1 result to review", and revision 2 as active; axe-core found no WCAG A/AA violations in the tab; no console errors. `tests/evidence/slice-5d/capability-lifecycle-browser.json`.
- `tests/recovery-drill.mjs` compares the restored governance read with the stored packages after removing read-only fields; it now also removes the four derived fields added here (`lifecycle`, `replacedByRevision`, `usage`, `impact`).
- The harnesses that transpile `governance.ts` (`governance`, `agent`, `ai-budget`, `provider-sandbox` and `agent-quality` contracts) now also list `capability-lifecycle`; their counts are unchanged.

## Limits

- Stages are derived from the existing draft/reviewed/active/suspended/retired states; there is no separate registration verification step, no "under review" state between evaluation and the review decision, and no trial or shadow operation that runs a capability without showing its results in care.
- Activation remains per organisation. There is no deployment-specific activation within an organisation (for example per site or per team beyond the package's team scope).
- The in-progress list is computed on read and shown on the Clinical governance page; it is not delivered as a notification or added to worker queues, and cancelling or reviewing still happens one request at a time in each visit, by people with care scope for that patient.
- BR-AI-04 stays partial.
