# ADR11 Operational metrics projection

Status: Proposed

## Decision

Build controlled report projections from versioned domain events.

## Rationale

Queries can disclose source completeness and avoid double counting.

## Consequences

Late records require reproducible metric revision.

Authority: BRD v1.1; High Level Design v1.0. Review owner: architecture lead with relevant clinical, privacy or integration owner. Acceptance remains open.
