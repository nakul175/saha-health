# ADR07 Immutable confirmed revisions

Status: Proposed

## Decision

Use append-only clinical fact revisions and approval hashes.

## Rationale

Corrections remain attributable and invalidate affected work.

## Consequences

Storage and retention policy must cover original and corrected versions.

Authority: BRD v1.1; High Level Design v1.0. Review owner: architecture lead with relevant clinical, privacy or integration owner. Acceptance remains open.
