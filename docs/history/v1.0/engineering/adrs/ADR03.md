# ADR03 Canonical operational model

Status: Proposed

## Decision

Persist typed operational entities and transform at adapter boundaries.

## Rationale

Shared facts retain provenance while destinations keep independent schemas.

## Consequences

Canonical shape is not itself an ABDM clinical document.

Authority: BRD v1.1; High Level Design v1.0. Review owner: architecture lead with relevant clinical, privacy or integration owner. Acceptance remains open.
