# ADR01 One encounter workspace

Status: Proposed

## Decision

Use a task-centred mobile flow with person and encounter context fixed.

## Rationale

Workers reuse facts across programmes; distinct patient records remain isolated.

## Consequences

UI widgets and question packs require usability validation.

Authority: BRD v1.1; High Level Design v1.0. Review owner: architecture lead with relevant clinical, privacy or integration owner. Acceptance remains open.
