Programme pack installation
Owner uploads · another clinician reviews · owner activates

Installed packs are synthetic configuration and are not clinically approved content. Review here checks structure, conflicts and authority only. Use synthetic test packs.

A pack manifest is data only: questions, shared measurements with their fixed units, answer options, show-when conditions, a follow-up interval and escalation rules in the protocol rule vocabulary. Optional completionRules declare required question evidence; referralNeeds declare conditions, urgency and an explicit compatibility key or separate referrals. These remain synthetic governed contracts, not clinical acceptance. Episodes open only on activated versions. Retiring a version keeps it and its pinned episodes; it stops new episodes and migrations to it. Once a new version of an existing pack is active, use Programme pack versions to migrate open episodes when the manifest declares a mapping.
